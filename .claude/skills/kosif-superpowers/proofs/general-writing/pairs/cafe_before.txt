Nestled in the heart of Zamalek, Bean & Brass isn't just a coffee shop — it's a love letter to Egyptian craft. Since opening its doors in 2019, owner Mona Hassan has meticulously curated a vibrant space where every cup tells a story.

The 24-seat café serves as a hub for creatives, showcasing beans roasted in-house from farms in Ethiopia and Yemen, highlighting its commitment to quality. Experts believe this marks a pivotal moment for Cairo's specialty coffee landscape.

**Why visit?**
- **Coffee:** A flat white costs 45 EGP.
- **Hours:** Open 8:00 to 23:00, closed on Fridays.
- **Location:** 12 Shagaret El Dor Street.

No laptops after 18:00. No reservations. No compromises. The future looks bright for Bean & Brass.
