# Copy

Short, specific, human. The page should sound like a person who makes things, not a model describing them.

- One thought per section. Headings of 3 to 7 words. One supporting line, rarely two.
- No em or en dashes. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
- No invented numbers, quotes, customers or awards. Real prices and real claims only; a fictional brand says so in
  the footer.
- Kill the giveaway words: delve, seamless, robust, unlock, elevate, leverage, game-changing, journey, realm,
  supercharge, effortless, cutting-edge.
- Kill the giveaway shapes: "not just X, but Y"; lists of exactly three adjectives; a closing line that sums up the
  section; every bullet starting with a bold word.
- Put a person back in: one concrete detail per claim ("Cold things first."), sentence lengths that vary,
  one plain rough edge a cautious writer would cut.
- Buttons say what happens: "Reserve yours", "Start a project", "Download for Mac". Not "Get started".

If a linter like SlopMonster is installed, run it on the built page and ship only at a perfect score.
