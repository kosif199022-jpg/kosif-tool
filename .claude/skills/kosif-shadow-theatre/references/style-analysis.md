# Reference analysis — shadow-theatre act on a TV talent show

Source supplied by the owner: «Attraction» (shadow theatre group) audition on *Britain's Got Talent*, 227 s, 1920×1080,
25 fps. Watched here frame by frame (contact sheet every 6 s) and measured with `shadow_theatre.py analyze`.
No frame, audio or choreography of the act is stored in this repository; this file keeps only observations.

## Measured

- 11 hard cuts (3.1, 9.1, 30.4, 32.0, 81.6, 135.4, 136.7, 204.6, 207.9, 209.9, 212.0 s). All are TV edits to the stage,
  judges or audience. Inside the act, the picture never cuts: it changes by bodies re-forming, blackout and iris.
- About 58 % of sampled frames are the lit screen (the rest are reaction shots and the bow). On the screen, the silhouette
  covers about 28 % of the central area on average.
- Screen palettes sampled at the top of the screen: deep blue-violet (16,16,98) → (50,74,148); centre and horizon:
  pale blue (109,167,227)–(152,186,254), teal (93,141,135)–(140,184,183), lime horizon on green-sky scenes, crimson
  fields with a white central pool. Clouds are soft, wide, horizontal.
- Silhouettes are true black with slightly soft edges; bodies near the lamp are larger and softer.

## What the skill takes from this (grammar, not content)

Black bodies as building blocks; changes without cuts; one light pool for intimacy; size = distance from the lamp;
shaped light windows; projected skies with a bright horizon band; a story with one emotional turn that ends on a child.
The demo «حارس الفانوس» uses this grammar for a different, original story (desert, lantern, palm, boat, crescent).
