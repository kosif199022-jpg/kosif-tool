# Revision checklist

Before re-exporting a revised edit, confirm each line on the new export itself (not on the timeline):

- [ ] The previous approved version is kept; the new one has a new version number.
- [ ] Only the requested changes were made; approved shots, timings and sound are untouched.
- [ ] Spoken words keep their absolute timing after any boundary shift; no stray frames from an adjacent take at B-roll returns.
- [ ] Grain moves with the picture every frame (no static overlay, no visible repeat); presenter shots stay clean.
- [ ] Bloom does not smear faces or captions; B&W is neutral.
- [ ] Captions: every word appears when spoken, lines justified, accents in italic serif, no jump across cuts, inside safe margins at the target size.
- [ ] Type layers: entrances/exits as specified, continuous motion does not reset at cuts, only the three font families are used.
- [ ] Splits: presenter re-cropped, not squeezed; mask edges clean on both sides of transitions.
- [ ] No missing media, black or frozen frames, bars or stretching.
- [ ] Duration, fps, resolution and audio match the brief; loudness checked (e.g. `tools/verify_video.py`).
- [ ] If music was added: a version without music is delivered as well.
- [ ] Report lists what changed, what was checked and any limitation honestly.
