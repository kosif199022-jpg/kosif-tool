# 86-style deep decomposition — source-grounded research map

The provided archive research report indexed 86 reference examples in 14 families and identified 112 *textual operator labels*. Those labels were provisionally grouped as follows, without claiming fundamental mathematical minimality:

1. Representation and encoding (8)
2. Sampling and measurement (6)
3. Constructing geometry (17)
4. Transforming coordinates (12)
5. Field propagation (9)
6. Combination and masking (7)
7. Imprinting material (12)
8. Lighting and optics (12)
9. Discretization and filtering (5)
10. Timing and revealing (12)
11. Constraints and topology (8)
12. Persistence and feedback (4)

**Caution:** This is a working analyst taxonomy. Group sizes sum to 112 label associations, not 112 individually proved executable engine functions.

Every one of the 86 cards should be represented as `{source_id, family, state, operation, rule, time, material_memory, research_feedback, typed_inputs, typed_outputs, evidence, tests}`. The deep audit is an analytical proposal until each style is reconstructed visually.

**Concrete mechanism example:** In “glass that remembers light”, passing light builds a persistent illumination field M; subsequent particles steer according to the spatial gradient of M. In the counterfactual control, the *same stimulus and field* exist but particle steering ignores M. A difference in trajectories after light stops establishes a causal response to history **in the numerical model only**.

**Status from audit, as research not production:** indexed 86/86 source cards; 112 analyst labels; 12 working families; 75 reproducible candidate *descriptions*. This does not verify 86 renders, 112 code modules, online/world originality, or autonomous learning.

**Update 2026-10-24:** the 86 style sources were decomposed from their code with REA and a lexical scan (`data/86_style_code_bricks.json`, see [code-bricks.md](code-bricks.md)); 95.7% of checkable analyst tags are supported by code. The glass example above now runs as recipe KMX-R-0001 / experiment EXP-0001 with three conditions, eight seeds and a dose sweep, and is rendered (see [engine.md](engine.md)).
