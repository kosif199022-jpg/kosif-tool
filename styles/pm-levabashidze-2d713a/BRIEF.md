## الفكرة
الشوريل يشرح مبادئ الموشن بأمثلة سريعة مرقمة كفصول (توقيت، تيسير، شكل، فضاء، أنظمة، خط، تصيير)، وكل فصل ثانية أو ثانيتان. الطابع واجهة برنامج تحريك: علامات زوايا، عداد زمني، أسماء أقسام صغيرة أحادية العرض، ومنحنيات بيزييه. التباين بين خلفيات سوداء وبرتقالية وكريمية وزرقاء يصنع الإيقاع، والخط غروتسك عريض ثقيل مع كلمة مائلة سيريف للمفاجأة.

## Concept
The reel teaches motion principles through quick numbered chapters (timing, easing, shape, space, systems, type, render), each a second or two. It wears the skin of animation software: corner marks, a running timecode, tiny monospace section labels and Bézier curves. Alternating black, orange, cream and blue backgrounds set the rhythm, and the type is a heavy wide grotesk with one italic serif word as a twist.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Timing: on near-black, an orange ball bounces along a tick-marked ruler, leaving faint onion-skin arcs; markers and an 'anticipation' callout label the squash before a jump.
- 1.8-3.6s: Easing: hard switch to orange; 'EASE IN.' slams in beside a curve-editor box, then 'EASE OUT.' with a new curve, then 'never LINEAR.' with an italic serif 'never' and a spring curve.
- 3.6-5.5s: Shape: cream ground with concentric thin rings; a black circle morphs into a blurred blue star blob, then an orange hexagon, each labelled small below.
- 5.5-7.4s: Space: a white isometric grid of cubes on black rises in a ripple with orange cubes forming a pattern, then the camera tilts and swoops close with motion blur.
- 7.4-9.3s: Systems: a black-on-cream interlocking curve tile pattern fills the frame, then tiles rotate and an orange variant glitches through in slices.
- 9.3-11.2s: Type: a dark wall of repeating 'design that moves' lines scrolls in alternating directions, mixed weights, with one orange line; the 'O' becomes a blue dot and the camera pushes in with blur.
- 11.2-12.8s: Render: electric-blue screen with a big percentage rolling up digit by digit and a filmstrip of the previous chapters filling a progress bar.
- 12.8-15s: End card on cream: a heavy black name with an orange dot, a rule, small labels and an italic serif tagline in orange.

## Motion rules
- Seven chapters in 15 s, about 1.5-2 s each, joined by hard cuts on the beat; within a chapter elements move fast with strong ease-out.
- Persistent HUD frame: corner brackets, top-left studio label, top-right reel label, bottom-left chapter number and name, bottom-right running timecode, all in tiny tracked monospace.
- Palette: near-black #121212, vermilion #e2512f, cream #f1ece3, violet blue #5a55e8, electric blue #2b3de8, white #ffffff.
- Type: heavy wide grotesk in caps for statements, a single italic serif word for contrast, monospace for all labels and code-like values.
- Demonstrate the principle literally: show the curve that drives the move, onion-skin the bounce, label the shape morphs.
- Motion blur and quick camera swoops on 3D and type walls; digits roll like an odometer in the render counter.
