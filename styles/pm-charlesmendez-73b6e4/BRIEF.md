## الفكرة
قصة منتج من أول سؤال يطرحه المشتري إلى الإيراد المغلق، تُروى عبر عميل واحد مثالي حتى يشعر المشاهد بالسلسلة كاملة. كل فصل يبدأ بعنوان قصير من سطرين يساراً ثم تتحرك الواجهة يميناً لتثبته. الأخضر يعني دائماً شيئاً يعمل أو نجح، والباقي أبيض ورمادي على أسود.

## Concept
A product story from the first question a buyer asks to closed revenue, told through one example customer so the viewer feels the whole chain. Each chapter opens with a short two-line headline on the left, then the UI moves on the right to prove it. Green always means something working or succeeding; everything else is white and grey on black.

- Palette: black #050605 with a faint green radial glow, panel #111412 with 1 px borders #1f2420, white #f4f6f4, grey #8a918c, one accent neon green #2cff3a (also as soft glows); the only other colour is a small yellow icon inside the ad card.

- Layout: headline left third, UI panel right two thirds with rounded corners; a thin green progress line along the bottom edge advances per chapter, with small monospace labels at the corners.
- UI builds piece by piece with 150-250 ms fade-and-rise staggers; numbers roll up; bars and lines grow with ease-out over ~600 ms; text fields type on.
- Pacing: a chapter about every 5-7 s, cut between chapters on a brief black or crossfade; holds long enough to read each panel.
- The logo end card builds from a green dot and bar, then the wordmark types in, held about 4 s.
