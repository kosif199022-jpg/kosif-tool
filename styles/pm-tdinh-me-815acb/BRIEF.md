## الفكرة
الشعار يُبنى أمام العين بدل أن يظهر جاهزاً: أولاً الفكرة (شبكة سلكية)، ثم المادة (مثلثات ملونة تأتي من كل اتجاه)، ثم الهوية (أيقونة واسم). الإيقاع صبور في البناء ثم سريع في الختام، وكل شيء في المنتصف على خلفية داكنة بتوهج خفيف.

## Concept
The logo is built in front of you instead of appearing finished: first the idea (a wireframe mesh), then the material (coloured triangles arriving from every direction), then the identity (icon and name). The pace is patient during the build and brisk at the end, all centred on a dark background with a soft glow.

- 0-3s: Black navy frame; thin grey-white edges and vertices draw outward from the centre and connect into a brain outline with a short stem.
- 3-6s: The wireframe holds with a slow drift; the first white facets snap onto the stem and lower lobes while a few loose shards tumble in from the edges.
- 6-13s: Assembly: triangles in white, light blue-grey, pink, coral and peach fly in on curved paths with spin, each landing with a tiny settle; the fill climbs from bottom to top until the mesh disappears under the facets.
- 13-14.5s: Completed brain glows; a thin ring expands and fades around it with a warm radial bloom.
- 14.5-16s: The brain shrinks into a rounded-square app icon with a dark plum-to-navy gradient and soft shadow.
- 16-21s: The icon slides left and scales down; a coral caret appears and the wordmark types on letter by letter in bold white sans, then a small grey tagline fades in beneath; hold.
