## الفكرة
الخدمة تحوّل معرفة شركة صغيرة المبعثرة إلى «عقل ثانٍ» مرتب، والفيلم يروي ذلك كرحلة من أربع مراحل مرقمة على شريط تقدم سفلي ثابت: تدقيق، نسخة عمل، اختبارات استرجاع، تسليم. البداية فوضى على ورق فاتح، والوسط طرفية داكنة نظيفة تمثل النظام المرتب، والنهاية عودة إلى الورق الفاتح بعبارة الوعد. لكل مرحلة لون (برتقالي، بنفسجي، أزرق، أخضر) يتكرر في الشريط والمربعات والشارات.

## Concept
The service turns a small business's scattered knowledge into an organised 'second brain', and the film tells it as a four-stage journey numbered on a fixed bottom progress rail: audit, working copy, retrieval tests, handoff. It opens on messy evidence on light paper, moves into a clean dark terminal that stands for the organised system, and returns to the light paper for the promise line. Each stage owns a colour (orange, violet, blue, green) repeated in the rail, the logo squares and the status chips.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-0.6s: Blank off-white page; four tiny coloured squares (orange, violet, blue, green) sit centre as a logo seed.
- 0.6-2.5s: Faded evidence cards fade in around the edges; a bold dark headline types up in a wavy settle saying it is not a prompting problem, then the key word is struck through and swaps to 'context' in orange.
- 2.5-5.5s: The headline shrinks away; a stage label and an audit counter tick from 00 to 09 top-centre while orange conflict lines draw between cards, red not-equal badges pop on them and small orange 'conflict' and green tags stamp on; the bottom rail fills stage 01.
- 5.5-7.5s: A black bar expands into a dark terminal panel; a monospace folder tree types in on the left, then a decision note on the right shows a service price with the old price in red being struck and the new one marked active.
- 7.5-10.5s: Stage 03: a question prompt types at the top of the panel, the answer price appears with a green pass chip, then a list of about eight retrieval checks ticks green one by one with a footer line.
- 10.5-12s: The panel collapses to a small card labelled with a handoff folder, then shrinks to a black sliver; the rail reaches stage 04 in green; the four squares scatter as confetti.
- 12-15s: Kicker in tracked mono with the four squares, a two-line bold headline about delivering second brains slides up word by word, a four-colour underline, then everything collapses back to the four squares.

## Motion rules
- Palette: paper #f3f1ec, ink #1d1d1f, stage colours orange #e5532a, violet #7b4fd6, blue #3b5fe0, green #2fae63; terminal panel #111214 with muted grey mono text and green #34c46d pass chips.
- Type: heavy grotesk sans for headlines (dark, tight tracking), tiny tracked uppercase monospace for kickers, stage labels and terminal content.
- A four-stage progress rail is always visible at the bottom; its dot and label for the active stage fill in colour as each section starts.
- Words enter with a quick upward settle (about 300 ms, slight overshoot); a replaced word is struck through first, then swapped.
- Conflict lines draw on as thin orange strokes (about 400 ms) with badges popping at their midpoints; counters tick in fixed-width mono.
- Panels expand from and collapse to a black bar or card with a smooth ease-in-out (about 500 ms); no hard cuts, everything morphs.
- Pacing: one new idea about every 2.5 s across 15 s; the four squares bookend the film.
