## الفكرة
الفكرة أن يعرض مصمم الموشن أدواته الأساسية كدروس مصغرة متتالية، كل واحدة في ثانية أو أقل: منحنيات التسارع، مبادئ الضغط والمط، الأنظمة والحقول، الجسيمات والعمق، البيانات، والشكل. الأسلوب نظيف وتقني يشبه دفتر ملاحظات مصمم: خطوط مرجعية رفيعة، ملصقات مائلة صغيرة، ومسطرة توقيت في الأسفل، مع لوحة ألوان أساسية من الأزرق والأبيض والأصفر والأحمر.

## Concept
The idea is a motion designer showing their core tools as back-to-back mini lessons, each about a second long: easing curves, squash and stretch, systems and fields, particles and depth, data, and form. The style is clean and technical like a designer's notebook: thin reference lines, small italic labels and a timing ruler at the bottom, with a primary palette of blue, white, yellow and red.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.2s: Royal blue with thin guide lines: 'MOTION' letters fall in one by one and bounce into place, the O landing as a ring; six small easing graphs (linear, ease in-out, expo out, back out, elastic, bounce) draw their curves underneath.
- 1.2-2.9s: A white circle wipe opens to off-white: a blue ellipse, then a red square and a yellow triangle each arc and land on a floor line with squash and stretch, onion-skin ghosts and dotted trajectory points, with impact ticks on landing.
- 2.9-3.7s: Yellow: a dot grid appears, then turns into short black, red and blue dashes that rotate to follow a swirling flow field.
- 3.7-6.3s: Blue: a white dot grid scatters into coloured particles that stream together to form 'flow' between typographic guide lines (ascender, x-height, baseline), then reshape into a 3D sphere, a torus, and collapse to a single horizontal line.
- 6.3-7.8s: Off-white: a big rolling frame counter and a percentage ring fill while black bars grow and a line chart draws over them; the last bar is red; bars then recolour in the palette.
- 7.8-8.4s: Dark grey: 'shape' in outlined letters with a red dot and slight chromatic glitch.
- 8.4-10s: Blue end card: 'Claude' in bold white grotesk with RGB split resolving, a red dot, an italic serif 'motion designer' and a tiny caption, framed by typographic guide lines.

## Motion rules
- Palette: royal blue #0d276e, off-white #f2f4f7, yellow #f6c51f, red #e02826, near-black #1a1a1f.
- Type: bold geometric grotesk for big words, italic serif for small labels and the role line, tiny italic captions per section, all on visible type guide lines.
- Every section is 0.6-1.7 s and demonstrates one named principle; a thin timeline ruler with red tick marks runs along the bottom throughout.
- Physics-flavoured motion: drops with bounce and elastic settle, arcs with squash and stretch, particles driven by flow fields and morph targets (word, sphere, torus, line).
- Transitions are graphic: circle wipes, colour field swaps and a brief RGB split; no fades longer than about 200 ms.
- Thin 1px guide lines, dotted trajectories and small labels give a 'working notes' feel over clean flat colour.
