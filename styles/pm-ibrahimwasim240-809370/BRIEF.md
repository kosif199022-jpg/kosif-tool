## الفكرة
السيرة الذاتية تُروى كلعبة: كل مهارة مستوى، والنقاط والوقت يعدّان في شريط علوي ثابت كواجهة لعبة حقيقية، والبطل الصغير الأخضر يربط المستويات. التصميم الجرافيكي، تطوير البرمجيات، تطوير الويب، وإدارة المشاريع تتحول إلى ميكانيكيات لعب مفهومة بنظرة، ثم تُختم بنتيجة اللعبة: شخصية بمستوى 99 وكل المهارات في أقصاها.

## Concept
The CV is told as a game: each skill is a level, score and timer count in a fixed top HUD like a real game, and a small green hero links the levels. Game design, software, web and project management each become a gameplay mechanic readable at a glance, and it closes on the game's result: a level-99 character with every skill maxed.

## Motion rules
- Fixed game HUD on every level: score counter top-left that keeps climbing, level dots and label top-right, a countdown timer bottom-right and a level caption bottom-left.
- One level per ~2.5-3 s with hard cuts or quick pixel-block wipes; the hero sprite carries over between levels.
- Everything snaps on a pixel grid: stepped movement, no smooth rotation; popups (+100, +5000) rise and fade in ~400 ms.
- Glitch transitions on the title (RGB split and tile shuffle) and a zoom-through-door transition into the finale.
- Neon palette on indigo: background #0e0a26, purple #7b4dff, hot pink #ff2e88, lime #c6ff3d, cyan #36d6ff, coin yellow #ffb02e.
- Type: a dot-matrix pixel display face for titles and HUD, a heavy condensed sans for banners with a lime outer glow, monospace in the code editor.
- UI panels (editor, browser, kanban) use dark rounded cards with thin neon borders so they read as in-game screens.
