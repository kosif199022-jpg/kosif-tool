## Concept
The subject is a platform that orchestrates coding agents from planning to release, and the film tells it as a chain of short arguments: the bottleneck moved, one continuous line of work, a shared brain plus an execution layer, and verification inside the loop. The look is a calm desktop UI: greyish white, heavy black type, fine grey detail, and a single lime accent that means 'active' or 'done'. Each scene opens with one headline line and then builds a simple diagram beneath it.
