## الفكرة
الفكرة أغنية ساخرة عن مخاوف الذكاء الاصطناعي تتحول إلى رسوم كرتونية بأسلوب كتب الأطفال: كل سطر من الأغنية مشهد مستقل بخلفية ألوان مائية مختلفة، والشخصيتان ثابتتا التصميم في كل المشاهد. ميزان حرارة جانبي يعرض نسبة 'الخطر' ويرتفع مع تقدم الأغنية، وكلمات مثل CHOMP وBOOM تُرسم بخط كوميكس أصفر.

## Concept
A satirical song about AI fears becomes a children's-book cartoon: each lyric line is its own scene on a different watercolour backdrop, while the two characters keep a consistent design throughout. A side thermometer shows a rising 'doom' percentage as the song progresses, and sound-effect words are lettered in a yellow comic hand.

- 60-80s: A crowned green serpent bursts through a stage floor with a yellow BOOM and flying planks; a cosmic turntable deck with a number counter; a row of box creatures wired as a neural network with pink lines beside the scientist.
- 80-105s: Desert sunset road with cacti and a curve sign; a car driving off a cliff edge at night; a cave tunnel spinning inward; a tiny island with a palm on a grey planet covered in paper clips.

## Motion rules
- One scene per lyric line (~5-8 s each), hard cuts or quick wipes on the line change; inside a scene, characters bob, blink and gesture on a gentle 2-4 frame hold (limited animation feel).
- Everything has watercolour wash backgrounds and paper grain, with thick dark-brown outlines #2b1f1a and flat fills on characters.
- Character sheet: box creature orange #e27a4a with darker edges, dot eyes and an arc smile, four stick legs and stubby arms; scientist with round glasses, dark messy hair, white lab coat and blue trousers.
- Lyric captions sit bottom-centre in a rounded dark pill #3a2f3f with a bold rounded sans in cream #f4e6c8 and selective yellow #f2c84b highlight on key words.
- A 'doom' thermometer appears on the right in several scenes, its fill and percentage climbing over the video.
- Sound-effect words in a chunky hand-lettered comic font, yellow #f2b632 with a darker shadow, pop in with a 1.2 to 1.0 scale bounce.
- Palette per scene changes freely (navy night #2b3466, sunset orange #f3a35a, bubblegum pink #f6b6c8, teal #4aa39a, curtain red #c8343a) but character colours never change.
