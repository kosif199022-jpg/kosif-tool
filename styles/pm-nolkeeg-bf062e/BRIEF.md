## الفكرة
الفكرة تمطيط ثانية واحدة إلى 45 ثانية: كل فصل يأخذ حدثاً كونياً أو إنسانياً يحدث خلال ثانية (الضوء، دوران الأرض، البرق، الشمس، القلب، المواليد) ويعرضه كرقم ضخم يعدّ أمام المشهد. شريط زمني دقيق أسفل الإطار يتقدم من 0.000 إلى 1.000 ثانية ليذكّر أن كل هذا داخل لحظة واحدة. الطابع وثائقي فخم: سواد، إضاءة طبيعية، نص أبيض كبير مع سطر إيطالي رقيق تحته.

## Concept
The idea is to stretch one second into 45: each chapter takes a cosmic or human event that happens within a second (light, Earth's motion, lightning, the Sun, the heart, births) and shows it as a giant counting number in front of the scene. A precise timeline at the bottom of the frame advances from 0.000 to 1.000 s to remind that all of it fits in one instant. The tone is premium documentary: black, natural lighting, big white numerals with a delicate italic line beneath.

- 7-14s: Chapter 01, light: a rim-lit Earth with a flare; a big white number in km counts up fast as thin light rings wrap the globe; italic caption and small red mono lap counter below.

## Motion rules
- Pure black #000 with sparse dim stars; the only accent is a warm orange-red #e2552d for the timeline, chapter labels and the heartbeat line.
- Numbers in a heavy geometric sans, very large (~18% of frame height), white #f5f5f5, with a small unit suffix; counters ease-out over the whole chapter.
- Captions in an elegant serif italic beneath the number; tiny uppercase mono for labels like '01 — LIGHT' and fine print.
- HUD: thin corner brackets, small header text, and a full-width hairline progress bar at the bottom with a moving marker and seconds readout (three decimals).
- Hero objects (Earth, Sun) are photoreal, rim-lit, slowly rotating; camera creeps (2–3% scale over a chapter) and transitions are soft fades through black or light blooms.
- Title treatment: white letters with film grain/noise texture and soft glow, fading in with slight blur-to-sharp.
- Pacing about 6 s per chapter; no hard cuts inside a chapter.
