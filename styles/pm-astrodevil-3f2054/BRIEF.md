## الفكرة
الموضوع وكالة تكتب المحتوى التقني وتدير علاقات المطورين لشركات أدوات البرمجة. الفيلم يبيعها بالأدلة: كل لقطة رقم واحد ضخم (مشاهدات، نجوم، ظهور، ترتيب) مع رسم صغير يشرحه. الأسلوب داكن وسينمائي، ولون واحد برتقالي أحمر يحمل كل التأكيد، ووسوم صغيرة متباعدة الحروف بخط ثابت العرض تعطي طابع الطرفية.

## Concept
The subject is an agency that writes technical content and runs developer relations for devtool companies. The film sells it with proof: each shot is one giant number (views, stars, impressions, rankings) with a small graphic explaining it. The look is dark and cinematic, a single red-orange carries all emphasis, and tiny tracked monospace labels give a terminal flavour.

## Motion rules
- Palette: warm black #0c0b0b with a large soft red-orange radial glow #8a2a14 in one corner; accent orange #ff5a2c; full-ground coral #ff6a45; text white #f2f0ee with a subtle grey gradient.
- Type: ultra-heavy tight-tracked grotesk for numbers and words, plus small widely tracked monospace caps for kickers, corner labels and section counters.
- Each beat is one hero number that counts up over ~0.8-1 s with a supporting mini-graphic (curve, star, pixel grid, bars) that draws in at the same time.
- Thin hairline frame marks and tiny mono labels sit in the corners of every shot like a HUD; a thin orange progress line runs along the bottom edge.
- Cuts every ~1.2-2 s; two or three beats invert to a full orange ground for punch.
- Words enter with a quick upward slide and blur-to-sharp (about 250 ms); the end wordmark types on with a block cursor.
- Fine film grain and a few slow dust particles keep the black from looking flat.
