## الفكرة
كل مشهد معادلة واحدة تتحول إلى صورة، وكل إطار حرفياً دالة في الزمن. الإطار الخارجي يشبه لوحة أداة علمية: عداد زمن، رقم المشهد، والمعادلة أسفل اليسار. الألوان نيون (فيروزي، وردي، برتقالي، بنفسجي) على أسود، والانتقالات سريعة بنبض مثل عرض أعمال.

## Concept
Each scene is one equation turned into a picture, and every frame is literally a function of time. The outer frame looks like a scientific instrument panel: a time readout, a scene counter and the governing formula bottom-left. Neon colours (cyan, pink, orange, violet) on black, with showreel-fast transitions.

- 16.5-20s: Euler's identity builds term by term beside a unit circle with an orange arc sweeping to minus one; it shrinks up as a wide-tracked wordmark and small subtitle fade in.

## Motion rules
- Background black #07080c with a very faint vignette; HUD frame of hairlines in #2a2f3a with tiny tracked labels, a running time readout bottom-right and an 'n / 08' scene counter top-right.
- Neon palette: cyan #3cc9d6, pink #ff3d8b, orange #ffae2b, violet #9b5cff, blue #4a6bff; strokes 2-3 px with additive glow.
- Everything is computed from t: curves draw progressively via parametric sampling, never as pre-made paths.
- Each scene's governing formula sits bottom-left in an italic math serif; numeric readouts update live in tiny monospace.
- Transitions every ~2-3 s: HUD glitch/scanline flashes, circular reticle wipes or hard cuts; about eight scenes in 20 s.
- Fills arrive after outlines finish (outline draw → gradient fill over ~0.5 s).
- End card: wide-tracked thin capitals with a short gradient underline, small tracked subtitle and an italic tagline; audio present but not analysed.
