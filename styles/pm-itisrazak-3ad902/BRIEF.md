## الفكرة
الموضوع أداة تأخذ نصاً أو كتاباً وتخرج منه فيديو شرح مرسوماً باليد بصوت راوٍ وبلغات متعددة. الفيلم يبرهن على ذلك بمثال واحد يتحول أمام العين: فقرة عن دورة الماء تُظلَّل كلماتها، ثم تصبح رسماً بخط اليد يُرسم داخل مشغل فيديو مع ترجمة تتلون كلمة كلمة. لغة الحركة ورقية هادئة: خلفية بيضاء دافئة، خط سيريف للنص، خط عريض للعناوين والأرقام، وقسمان كحليان داكنان للغات والإحصاء.

## Concept
The subject is a tool that takes a text or a book and produces a hand-illustrated explainer video with narration in many languages. The film proves it with one example transforming on screen: a paragraph about the water cycle gets its key words highlighted, then becomes a hand-drawn diagram that draws itself inside a video player while a caption fills word by word. The motion language is calm and paper-like: warm white background, serif body text, heavy sans for headlines and numbers, and two deep-navy sections for languages and stats.

## Motion rules
- Prove the product with one transforming example: highlighted text becomes a drawing, the drawing becomes a playing video.
- Highlighter wipes run left to right in about 300 ms behind words, in pastel blue #6fa0ff, pink #f4a6c8 and green #9be8a0.
- Diagram strokes draw on with stroke-dashoffset in a causal order (sun, rays, vapour, cloud, sea), labels write in a handwritten marker face.
- Captions fill word by word in sync with an assumed narration; the language stack scrolls vertically with motion blur.
- Three hard cuts (about 6.7, 28.3 and 36.7 s) swap paper sections for navy ones; inside sections use push-ins and zoom-outs on cards.
- Palette: paper #fffffd, navy #1a1c2f, grey text #a3a2af, plus yellow #ffd21f for the sun.
- Type: Georgia-like serif for body, heavy geometric sans (800) for titles and counters, handwritten marker for diagram labels; thin HUD corner labels frame every shot.
