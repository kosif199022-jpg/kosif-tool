## الفكرة
الفكرة شخصية تقدّم نفسها بثلاث صفات وأربعة أشياء تفعلها، وكل صفة تُعرض بأسلوب بصري يجسدها: الفضول خربشة حرة، والصرامة شبكة دقيقة، والصراحة سيريف بسيط على أبيض بلا زينة. النقطة البرتقالية هي التوقيع الثابت: تبدأ القلم، وتصبح نقطة حرف i، ونقطة نهاية الجمل، والفاصلة العشرية في رقم النسخة. الإيقاع سريع (أقل من ثانيتين لكل صفة) والانتقالات تبدل لون الخلفية.

## Concept
The idea is a character introducing itself through three traits and four things it does, each trait shown in a visual treatment that embodies it: curiosity as a free scribble, rigour as a precise grid, candour as a plain serif on white with no decoration. The orange dot is the constant signature: it starts the pen, becomes the i's tittle, the full stop of each line and the decimal point in the version number. The pace is quick (under 2 s per trait) and transitions flip the background colour.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-2s: Indigo: an orange dot pulls a thin white line upward that loops and wanders into a dense tangled scribble.
- 2-3.3s: Hard cut to lavender: a stack of indigo horizontal rules slides across, the bold lowercase 'curious' sits on the baseline, the dot bouncing in as its tittle.
- 3.3-5s: Indigo with faint layout grid lines: 'rigorous' builds letter by letter in pale lavender, the dot snapping onto the i.
- 5-5.8s: White: 'candid' in a black book serif with an orange dot on the i, a small italic '(no filter)' fades in beneath.
- 5.8-7.4s: Lavender: a right-aligned list scrolls upward, the active line in bold indigo with an orange full stop, previous lines fading to pale lavender.
- 7.4-8s: A blurred indigo disc expands from the centre while the list warps and scatters, wiping to indigo.
- 8-10s: Indigo end card: big pale lavender two-line name with an orange decimal point; an underline draws from left and hooks into a small tagline beneath.

## Motion rules
- The orange dot #f26a3d is the only accent colour and appears in every beat as pen tip, tittle, full stop or decimal point.
- Three grounds rotate by hard cut: indigo #2420a8, lavender #d8dcf8 and white #ffffff; text is lavender on indigo and indigo or black on light grounds.
- Each trait gets its own visual metaphor (scribble, rules, grid, plain serif) held for 0.8-1.6 s.
- Type: rounded geometric sans in bold lowercase for most words; one book serif moment for the 'plain' trait; a thin small sans for the tagline.
- The dot lands with a small squash and 2-3 px overshoot; letters build in from the left at ~40 ms per glyph.
- The scroll list moves up one line per ~0.4 s with ease-in-out, inactive lines dropping to ~35% opacity.
