## Concept
The subject is a satirical pop song that tells the rise of an AI model like a pop idol's career, from early training days to the era of agents and huge compute. Each song section becomes its own printed panel: a close-up, a group dance, a rising chart, a giant counter or a tongue-in-cheek symbol. The date stamp advances with the story and a small percentage in the corner creeps upward like a worry meter, so the clip works as a timeline that is both playful and uneasy.
