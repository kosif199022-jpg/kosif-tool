## الفكرة
الفكرة عرض اتساع أعمال الوكالة: كل علامة تحصل على لحظة قصيرة بلونها وطابعها، والانتقال بينها هو القصة. لا يوجد قالب ثابت إلا الإيقاع والتمركز في المنتصف؛ كل لقطة تُبنى بطريقة مختلفة (رسم خطوط، نقاط رقمية، زجاج شفاف، كتابة بحث، تركيب صور) ثم تُقطع إلى التالية. الإيقاع يبدأ معتدلاً ثم يتسارع إلى ومضات.

## Concept
The idea is to show the agency's range: each brand gets a short moment in its own colour and mood, and the handoff between them is the story. There is no fixed template beyond rhythm and centring; each card builds differently (stroke draw, binary dots, clear glass, a typed search, photo mosaics) and then cuts to the next. The pace starts moderate and accelerates into flashes.

## Motion rules
- One brand per full-frame card, mark centred; each card has its own flat background colour and the cut itself is the transition (about 50 visual changes in 25 s).
- Accelerating rhythm: cards hold about 1-1.5 s early, about 0.5 s in the middle, and flash every ~150 ms in the 21-23 s burst.
- Each mark builds with its own technique: stroke draw-on, binary-digit fill, glass refraction, typed text, letter-swap, sliding rounded tiles.
- Photo cards use full-bleed photography with bold white condensed or cut-out type over it.
- Type is mostly geometric or grotesk sans in black or white, with a serif only on the letter card.
- Background colours seen: olive #5d6250, black #000000, sky #7fc6ef, sage #dfe7df, cream #f6f3ea, teal #2f7a6d, electric blue #1f2de0, deep green #1b4d36, mint #d5ecd9, lime #e8f20f.
- Trim the render: content ends at about 25 s; the remaining ~10 s are black.
