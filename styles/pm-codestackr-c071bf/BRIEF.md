## الفكرة
الريل يقدّم مصمم موشن متخيلاً عبر فصول قصيرة مرقمة، كل فصل مبدأ: الأنظمة (الإيقاع والتكرار والتموج)، الأبعاد (العمق والمدار)، التوقيت (التسارع عاطفة)، ثم الخط والحلقات. الإطار كله مؤطر كشاشة مونتاج بزوايا وعداد زمني وأرقام إطارات صغيرة، ما يعطي إحساس «ورقة مواصفات حية». ثلاثة ألوان فقط تتناوب بقوة: أسود، كريمي، برتقالي، مع أزرق كهربائي كلون مفاجأة.

## Concept
The reel presents an imagined motion designer through short numbered chapters, each one a principle: systems (rhythm, repetition, ripple), dimension (depth, orbit), timing (easing as emotion), then type and loops. The whole frame is dressed like an editing viewer with corner marks, a timecode and tiny frame numbers, giving a 'living spec sheet' feel. Three colours alternate hard (black, cream, orange) with electric blue as the surprise colour.

## Motion rules
- Fast cadence: a new idea every ~0.6 s in the opening word slams, then ~2 s per chapter; transitions are hard cuts with full background colour flips.
- Words enter with heavy motion blur and a scale/slide overshoot, or build letter by letter with masks; speed lines streak horizontally behind verbs.
- Persistent viewer chrome: thin corner brackets, a small timecode top-right and a frame/fps counter bottom-right on every shot.
- Chapters are labelled bottom-left with a red number, a heavy uppercase title and a serif-italic sub-caption.
- Procedural visuals (dot grid ripple, particle sphere to helix, eased bars) move continuously; nothing holds still except the final 1.5 s end card.
- Palette: black #0b0b0b, cream #efe9df, orange #ff5a1f, electric blue #2f4bff, peach #f2a58e, lime pill #b8f03a.
- Type: ultra-heavy extended grotesk for slams, a serif italic for connecting words, condensed mono for counters and labels.
