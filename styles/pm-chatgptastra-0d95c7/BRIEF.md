## الفكرة
الشوريل درس صغير في الحركة: كل مبدأ يظهر مطبقاً ثم يُعلَّق عليه بخط يد أزرق كأن المصمم يشرح على الشاشة. الخلفية تتبادل بين أبيض رمادي ناعم للشرح وأسود فحمي للأداء، والأزرق هو لون الحركة الصحيحة والأحمر لون الخطأ أو التأكيد. شريط زمني رفيع أسفل الشاشة يمشي عبر فصول المبادئ.

## Concept
The reel is a tiny motion lesson: each principle is shown in action and then annotated in blue handwriting as if the designer were explaining on screen. The background alternates between soft off-white for explanation and charcoal for performance; blue is the colour of good motion and red the colour of mistakes or emphasis. A thin timeline at the bottom advances through the principle chapters.

- 7.9-10.6 ث: شبكة نقاط على الفحمي: مذنب أزرق من النقاط ينطلق وذيله يتأخر ويلتف، ونقاط الشبكة الحمراء والزرقاء تتموج حوله؛ ملاحظة «متابعة»؛ والشبكة تميل في ثلاثة أبعاد.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.7s: Off-white with a soft vignette and a ground line: a blue ball arcs and bounces leaving outlined ghost circles spaced close at the ends and wide in the middle; handwritten notes with arrows label slow in/out, squash (red) and stretch.
- 1.7-3.5s: The ball becomes the full stop of a huge black condensed headline whose letters fly in, rotate and settle with a 45 ms stagger note; a blue hand-drawn underline swipes under it and the dot wobbles with an 'overlap' note.
- 3.5-5.4s: Charcoal: motion words perform themselves in white condensed caps (one bounces with gravity, one squeezes with smear blur), then 'SNAP' in red locks to red guide lines with the note that it has no ease on purpose.
- 5.4-7.9s: Off-white graph: a position-time line with red keyframes and an evenly spaced row of red outlined boxes below; a red note calls linear robotic, gets struck through, and the line bends into a blue S-curve while the boxes bunch at the ends; blue 'ease in-out' tick and 'spacing!' note.
- 7.9-10.6s: Charcoal dot grid: a blue comet of dots whips across, its tail lagging and curling, red and blue grid dots ripple around it; 'follow-through' note; the grid tilts in 3D.
- 10.6-12.6s: A circular loading ring with tick marks fills from 29% to 76% around a blue core and small shapes; then the core squashes flat to a blue ellipse with an 'anticipation' note at 100%.
- 12.6-15s: Off-white end card: a small condensed role line and a huge name assemble with scattered letters snapping into place, blue dot full stop and a handwritten 'open to new projects' note with an arrow.

## Motion rules
- Palette: paper #efefef with a soft grey vignette, charcoal #1d1f24, ink black #151515, motion blue #2f6df6, correction red #ef3b4a, white #f5f5f5.
- Type: ultra-condensed heavy sans (Anton or Bebas-like) for headlines and motion words; a casual marker handwriting font in blue or red for annotations, always with a hand-drawn arrow to its target.
- Annotations write on in ~300 ms after the action they describe; corrections are struck through rather than removed.
- Every principle is literally applied: real squash and stretch on the ball, 45 ms letter stagger, linear vs ease-in-out comparison with spacing ghosts, lagging trail for follow-through, squash before release for anticipation.
- A thin bottom timeline with diamond chapter markers and a running playhead labelled with the current principle; a tiny timecode top-right.
- Chapters last about 2-2.5 s and alternate light and dark backgrounds with quick wipes or cuts; end card holds about 1.5 s.
