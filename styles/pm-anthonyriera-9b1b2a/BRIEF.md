## الفكرة
التميمة هي الراوي والمؤشر معاً: تقفز من زاوية إلى أخرى لتقود العين، وتغيّر تعبير وجهها، وتتحول في النهاية إلى رسم خطي متقطع. الفيلم يشرح المنتج كقصة بثلاث مراحل: تجهيز الحسابات وتدفئتها، كتابة محتوى يبدو بشرياً، ثم التشغيل التلقائي في متصفح حقيقي، وينتهي بالفائدة: الظهور في البحث وفي إجابات الذكاء الاصطناعي. كل شيء على الأسود مع أحمر برتقالي واحد وأبيض.

## Concept
The mascot is both narrator and pointer: it hops from corner to corner to lead the eye, changes its facial expression, and ends up as a dashed line drawing. The film explains the product as a three-stage story: set up and warm up accounts, write content that reads human, then run on autopilot in a real browser, ending on the payoff of showing up in search and in AI answers. Everything is on black with one red-orange and white.

## Motion rules
- The mascot leads every scene: it squash-and-stretch hops (~300 ms) to the next focal point and lands before the content appears there.

- UI is recreated as flat dark cards: lists fill row by row, bars fill in segments, checklists tick in green, cursors move with ease-in-out and click with a small scale dip.
- Scene changes are mostly cuts on black with generous empty space; one glitchy scanline flash marks the shift to automation.
- Palette: black #0a0a0a, mascot red-orange #ff4500, dark reds #5a1a10 / #8a2a18 for pixel art, white #f5f5f5, grey #9a9a9a, check green #3ecf6e, pastel avatars (blue, pink, yellow, green, lilac).
- Type: a bold geometric sans for headlines, small regular sans for UI, monospace-like brackets for checklist items.
- Pacing is calm for a launch: ~4-6 s per idea with holds, finishing on a slow word-by-word sentence.
