## Concept
The subject is an update to a playful app that tracks the sweets you eat, and the film sells each feature with a short two-line joke: a first dark-brown line names the feature and a second pink line flips it with humour. Everything is round and soft: heavy rounded type, cards with thick outlines and a solid bottom shadow, and treats with faces that have feelings. Three backgrounds alternate (cream, chocolate, pink) to separate features, and the cookie mascot returns at the end to ask you to update.
