## الفكرة
الفكرة أن العميل المحتمل لا ينتظر، والمنصة ترد عليه فوراً عبر كل القنوات. اللغة البصرية سائلة وعضوية: القطرات والحبال والسوائل تمثل تدفق الرسائل، والليموني هو لون «الرد». كل مشهد فكرة واحدة بعنوان قصير ورسم واحد في المنتصف، مع وسوم صغيرة بخط أحادي في الزوايا كأنها واجهة تقنية.

## Concept
The idea is that a new lead does not wait, and the platform answers instantly on every channel. The visual language is liquid and organic: blobs, gooey strands and fluids stand for message flow, and lime is the colour of 'reply'. Each scene is one idea with a short headline and a single central graphic, with tiny mono labels in the corners like a technical interface.

## Motion rules
- Strict three-colour palette: charcoal #1b1b1b, off-white #efefea and acid lime #e2ff1f, with pure black #0d0d0d for blobs; no other hues.
- Liquid motion: metaball blobs that merge, stretch into strands and drip with springy overshoot; liquid fills slosh with small bubbles.
- Headlines in a wide extended bold grotesk, all caps, two lines, set bottom-left or top-left; tiny mono uppercase labels sit in corners and next to nodes.
- Lime full-frame cards punctuate the dark scenes (around 3-7 s and 20.6-22.5 s) like beats.
- Counters and scores count up fast with easing; stickers slap in with a tilt and overshoot.
- Particle sphere and wireframe cube rotate slowly in 3D; multilingual greetings drift and fade around them.
- Only one hard cut detected (~20.6 s); everything else transitions by morphs, wipes of liquid or colour floods.
