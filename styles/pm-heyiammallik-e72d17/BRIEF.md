## الفكرة
الفكرة أن يبيع المهندس نفسه بثلاث صفات تُقال بالخط قبل أي صورة (معقد، سريع، ينمو)، ثم يثبتها بالدليل: مشاهد ثلاثية الأبعاد من مشاريعه الحقيقية، أرقام تسريع ضخمة، قائمة إحدى عشرة تجربة، ثم أدوات ومواقع بناها. اللغة البصرية صارمة: أسود شبه تام، برتقالي محمر واحد للتأكيد، وأوف وايت دافئ لفاصل القائمة، وخط غروتسك عريض جداً مع وسوم أحادية المسافة صغيرة في الزوايا كأنها ملصقات تقنية.

## Concept
The engineer sells himself with three traits stated in type before any image (complex, fast, grows), then proves them: dark 3D scenes from real projects, giant speed-up numbers, a list of eleven pieces of work, then tools and sites he built. The visual language is strict: near-black, one orange-red accent, a warm off-white for the list interlude, and a very heavy grotesk with tiny monospace corner labels that read like technical tags.

## Motion rules
- Opening is a hard-cut salvo: about eight cuts in the first 3.25 s, then a slower 1-2 s cadence through the 3D and number sections, and long holds in the list and end card.
- Palette: near-black #060606, orange-red #c03d1f (vignetted to #ab331b at the edges), white #ffffff, warm off-white #e9e6e1 for the list interlude, mid grey #8a8a8a for inactive items.
- Type: one very heavy geometric grotesk for words and numbers (tight tracking, words end in an orange full stop or comma), a monospace for tiny uppercase corner tags, counters and the terminal.
- 3D scenes are dark, low-key and orange-lit: point clouds, voxels and wire grids under a slow camera drift; text never sits on a busy area without a dark falloff behind it.
- Numbers snap in whole rather than counting, except the pixel counter which rolls up; multipliers use a large 'x' at the same weight as the digits.
- List interlude: vertical scroll with the active row boxed in orange and inactive rows at about 35% opacity; a circular mask preview crossfades per row.
- Every frame carries a small corner index (section number or n / total) in mono at the top right, like a slide counter.
