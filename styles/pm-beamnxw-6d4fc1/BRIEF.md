## الفكرة
الفكرة إظهار خط إنتاج كامل لفيلم قصير ثلاثي الأبعاد في لقطة واحدة: القصة نفسها (شخصية مكعبات تلتقي روبوتاً صغيراً على لوحة مفاتيح، ويظهر قلب، ثم يقفز الروبوت فوق رأسها) تجري في نافذة المعاينة، وحولها الواجهة تشرح المرحلة الحالية: أي أداة نشطة، ما الذي تقوله التميمة، وما الأوامر في الطرفية. النتيجة مزيج من فيلم شخصيات لطيف ولوحة «كيف صُنع» حية.

## Concept
The idea is to show a whole short-film 3D pipeline in one frame: the story itself (a voxel character meets a tiny robot on a keyboard, a heart pops up, then the robot hops onto its head) plays in the preview window while the surrounding UI narrates the current stage: which tool is active, what the mascot says and which commands the terminal runs. It blends a cute character film with a live making-of dashboard.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-2s: Dashboard fully laid out on off-white; preview shows the voxel character beside a glass jar on a keyboard; the first tool card is highlighted and the terminal types the opening request.
- 2-4.5s: Building the world: the camera swings low across the keys as the character walks; the green robot scuttles between keys; the modelling card lights and the terminal shows scene code.
- 4.5-7s: Materials and animation: the glass jar refracts, the character steps along the number row, the mascot caption changes, and keyframe commands type out.
- 7-9.8s: Lighting and camera: the shot tightens on the jar over the Esc key with the robot under it; the lens and depth-of-field values type into the terminal.
- 9.8-12.5s: Payoff: the robot sits under the jar, the character leans in, a pixel heart bubble pops above, then the jar lifts and the robot hops onto the character's head.
- 12.5-15.6s: Export: the encode card lights, the terminal runs the final render command, the character smiles with the robot on top, and the mascot signs off.

## Motion rules
- Layout fixed for the whole piece: headline band on top, a 16:9 preview window in the middle third, a six-card tool row, a mascot plus a stage checklist, and a black terminal at the bottom.
- Palette: off-white UI #f6f4ef, coral red #e0604a for headline and character, near-black terminal #1d1f21, card grey #2a2d31, cream keycaps #efe6d6, robot green #3fae6a and warm brown desk #5a4436.
- The preview is a photoreal 3D render: shallow depth of field, soft warm key light, slow dolly and low orbit moves; the character moves in small hops with squash and stretch.
- The active tool card gets a thin outline and its mini-visual animates; the stage checklist item highlights in sync about every 2-3 s.
- The terminal types commands at about 40 characters per second with a blinking cursor; the mascot caption swaps per stage.
- No cuts in the UI; only the preview changes shots, with soft cuts between camera angles.
