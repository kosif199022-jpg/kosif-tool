## الفكرة
التطبيق يحوّل تجربة المكملات الغذائية إلى تجربة علمية على شخص واحد: تحدد القواعد مسبقاً، تسجل يومياً، وتحصل على حكم صريح ثم اقتراح التجربة التالية. الفيلم يبدأ بضجيج الإعلانات (حبوب تتطاير) ثم يهدأ إلى ورق كريمي وأخضر حكيم، فيشعر المشاهد بالانتقال من الدعاية إلى الدليل. الواجهة الحقيقية للتطبيق تظهر داخل هاتف، والأفكار تُشرح بعناصر واجهة مكبرة.

## Concept
The app turns supplement-taking into a single-person scientific trial: set the rules up front, log daily, and get an honest verdict and the next test to try. The film opens on advertising noise (pills flying) and then calms to cream paper and sage green, so the viewer feels the move from hype to evidence. The real app UI appears inside a phone, while ideas are explained with enlarged UI elements.

## Motion rules
- Palette: cream #f1ede4, ink #1f1f1d, sage green #4f7f5c, soft green tint #e3ebe2, white cards #ffffff with light shadow, black #0d0d0d for bookend scenes, accent gold #c9a24a for the opening stars.
- Type: a clean neo-grotesk in semibold for headlines, dark with the key word in green; a serif appears only inside the phone result screen.
- Calm product pacing: one sentence per scene, 3-4.5 s holds, soft ease-in-out moves of 500-800 ms; no hard glitches.
- UI elements are enlarged out of the phone: pill rows, a button, chart lines, nodes; they slide, stack and snap with gentle springs.
- Data is shown honestly: the chart highlights where the change happens, and the result reel lands on a single verdict.
- The arch logo is a recurring anchor: it travels beside sentences early and returns as the final mark.
- Bookend contrast: black, busy and blurred at the start; black and minimal at the end; cream and orderly in between.
