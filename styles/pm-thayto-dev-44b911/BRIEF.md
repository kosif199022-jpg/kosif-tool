## الفكرة
الموضوع أداة تكيّف السيرة الذاتية لكل وظيفة، والفيلم يبيع خطة Pro بالحساب لا بالمبالغة: كلما زادت الوظائف التي تتقدم لها، زاد ما تدفعه بالسعر الفردي، بينما يبقى سعر Pro ثابتاً. لغة الحركة لغة مستند مالي نظيف: خط سيريف انتقالي للعناوين والأرقام، واجهة بيضاء، أزرق واحد للتأكيد، صفوف تُضاف إلى قائمة ومجاميع تعدّ للأعلى، وشريحة داكنة واحدة تقلب الإيقاع قبل الختام.

## Concept
The subject is a tool that adapts a CV to each job, and the film sells its Pro plan with arithmetic rather than hype: the more jobs you apply to, the more the per-job price adds up, while the Pro price stays flat. The motion language is that of a clean financial document: a transitional serif for headlines and figures, white UI, a single blue for emphasis, rows appending to a list with totals counting up, and one dark slide that flips the rhythm before the close.

## Motion rules
- Calm pacing: long readable holds (about a third of the film is static) and only two hard cuts, into and out of the charcoal slide.
- Headlines enter line by line with a short fade and 10-16 px rise; the emphasised final line is the only blue text in the block.
- Numbers are the motion: job count, appended rows and the running total all tick up together so the price gap grows visibly.
- Cards build with 80-120 ms staggers and settle with an ease-out; no bounce, no rotation.
- Camera is static throughout; layout carries the story (left text plus right visual, then split, then grid, then centred).
- Palette: white #feffff, accent blues #4c76dd and #274eb1, charcoal about #1f1d1a for the dark slide, mint about #e8f6ec for savings notes and near-black #111111 text.
- Type: a transitional serif display for questions and prices, a small sans for UI labels.
