## الفكرة
شوريل موضوعه التسهيل والتحول: كيف تنتقل الأشياء من أ إلى ب. قلبه مشهد تعليمي صغير يضع ستة منحنيات تسهيل (خطي، دخول وخروج، أسي، تراجع، مرن، ارتداد) جنباً إلى جنب لتُرى الفروق. بقية الفصول تطبق الفكرة على أشكال وأنماط وأجسام ثلاثية الأبعاد وكلمات تُنطق بإيقاعها نفسه («تسارع»، «لا»، «خطي»). نظام ألوان مقيد: أسود، أحمر طماطم، كريمي، أزرق كهربائي، ورشة ليموني.

## Concept
A reel about easing and morphing: how things get from A to B. Its heart is a small teaching scene that lines up six easing curves (linear, ease-in-out, expo-out, back-out, elastic, bounce) side by side so the differences show. The other chapters apply the idea to shapes, patterns, 3D forms and words that perform their own meaning (accelerate, never, linear). A restricted colour system: black, tomato red, cream, electric blue and a splash of lime.

## Motion rules
- Palette: black #111111, tomato red #ef4b35, cream #f1ede4, electric blue #3434f2, lime #dcf53a, white; one dominant field colour per chapter.
- Type pairing: an ultra-wide heavy grotesk for the wordmark and kinetic words, an italic high-contrast serif for subtitles and titles, tiny monospace for HUD and labels.
- Words act out their meaning: an 'accelerate' word ramps in, a 'never' word flies erratically, a 'linear' word moves at exactly constant speed.
- Easing demo must use true curves: linear, cubic in-out, expo-out, back-out (overshoot ~10%), elastic (2-3 oscillations), bounce (3 bounces), all with equal duration (~1.2 s).
- Morphs are traced by thin red guide lines showing the in-between path; satellites morph in sync with the hero shape.
- Chapters cut hard on bar lines about every 1.9 s, with a faster cluster of cuts (~0.3 s) during the kinetic-words chapter.
- Constant HUD: corner brackets, chapter tag top-right, timecode and fps bottom-left, tempo and bar squares bottom-right, a thin progress rule.
