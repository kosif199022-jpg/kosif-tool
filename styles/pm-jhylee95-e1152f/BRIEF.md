## الفكرة
الميزات كثيرة، فالحل أن تُقال كل ميزة بكلمة واحدة: فعل قصير بحروف صغيرة وخط عريض جداً، ونقطة ملونة في نهايته كتوقيع العلامة، وتحته دليل صغير من الواجهة. الإيقاع صارم كنبض الموسيقى، والخلفية تتبدل مع كل كلمة بين أربعة ألوان فقط، فيبدو الفيديو قائمة ميزات سريعة لكنها مريحة للعين. النصف الثاني يقدّم الوكيل بنفس اللغة: كلمة، ثم واجهته وهي تعمل.

## Concept
There are many features, so each one is said in one word: a short verb in lowercase, very heavy type, with a coloured full stop as the brand signature and a small UI proof beneath it. The rhythm is strict like a music pulse, and the background switches with every word among only four colours, so the video reads as a fast but easy feature list. The second half introduces the agent in the same language: a word, then its interface at work.

- 1-6s: One word per second on alternating black, indigo and white: a selection box draws around one word with a dashed outline, a feature chip slides up under another, the letters of the voice word bounce, and the translate word cross-blurs between languages above a language chip.

- 14.9-20s: Agent demo on white: an assistant greeting, a user question, a thinking indicator, an answer with a linked step card, then the product's integrations page with a guided tooltip on a connect button; a motion-blur slide moves to a chart page.

- Strict one-second grid: a hard cut on almost every whole second (about 19 cuts), with the agent demo (15-20 s) as the only long continuous stretch.
- Palette: off-white #f5f5f7, black #010005, indigo #434ce7, teal #0f9483, white UI cards #ffffff, yellow dot #f5c84b (estimated) on the brand beat, green online dot.
- Type: an extra-bold geometric sans in lowercase, tight tracking, each word ending in a round full stop coloured to contrast the background (indigo on light, black on indigo, indigo on black).
- Each word gets exactly one micro-demo that animates within its second: tooltip pop, dashed selection draw, chip slide-up, letter bounce, cross-blur language swap, cursor click, typed code, staggered stat cards.
- Persistent brand chrome: a small round logo badge bottom-left and a small feature icon top-right that changes per word.
- UI pops use a quick scale from 90% with a soft shadow over about 200 ms; the cursor is a system arrow or hand that clicks with a small press.
