## الفكرة
الفكرة أن ملاحظة مكتوبة على عجل من فني صيانة تحتوي أعمالاً لم تدخل الفاتورة، والأداة تقرأ النصين معاً وتكشف الفرق. الفيديو مبني كمقال بصري: جملة عنوان قصيرة في منتصف شاشة فارغة (نصفها أسود ونصفها ملوّن بالأزرق أو الأحمر)، ثم دليل من داخل الواجهة، ثم جملة جديدة. الأزرق للفهم والمال المسترد، والأحمر للخطأ والمال الضائع، والكاميرا تتحرك ببطء داخل نافذة متصفح نظيفة.

## Concept
The idea is that a hastily typed field-technician note contains work that never reached the invoice, and the tool reads both texts side by side to expose the gap. The video is built like a visual essay: a short headline line centred on an empty screen (half black, half tinted blue or red), then proof from inside the interface, then the next line. Blue stands for understanding and recovered money, red for mistakes and lost money, and the camera drifts slowly inside a clean browser window.

- 10-17s: A horizontal glitch-smear wipe, then a black-and-red line stating the work was done but not billed, followed by a tiny typed 'intro' label.

- 24-44s: Inside the app: a browser window with a sidebar list of work orders; the camera pushes into the note/invoice pair, then into a list of checks with thin blue progress bars, then a second example where a suspicious note is blocked and marked in red.
- 44-57s: Second headline, then cards for uncertain cases with percentage confidence, highlighted yellow phrases and Bill it / Leave it buttons; a cursor clicks Leave it and the button fills black.
- 57-74s: Blur transition to an auto-bill settings panel with a slider, then an overview dashboard; the camera zooms to a giant blue dollar total that then sits alone with small coloured stat chips.

## Motion rules
- Canvas #f6f9fc with oversized pale tally-mark glyphs (#e3ebf5) drifting slowly behind everything; ink #111418, accent blue #1f5fd6, alarm red #d9463b, dark card #121418.
- Headlines in a clean grotesk at medium weight, split into a black clause and a coloured clause; body copy and notes in a small monospace.
- Typing reveals at a steady ~20 characters/s with a block caret; underlines draw left to right in about 0.4 s after the sentence finishes.
- Camera moves are slow eased push-ins and pans inside a browser frame (2–4 s each, ease-in-out); never a hard zoom.
- Transitions alternate between a soft cross-fade, a horizontal RGB smear/glitch wipe and a full-frame blur dissolve (~0.5 s).
- Numbers count up with a fast ease-out; money found is blue, money lost or blocked is red.
- A real-looking arrow cursor moves with eased paths and briefly scales down on click; buttons invert to solid black when chosen.
