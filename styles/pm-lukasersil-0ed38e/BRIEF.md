## الفكرة
البناء تصاعدي من البسيط إلى المعقد كدرس تصميم: نقطة، ثم خط، ثم مستوى، ثم حجم، ثم سائل، ثم جسيمات، ثم الحركة نفسها. الألوان الأساسية الثلاثة للباوهاوس (أزرق وأحمر وأصفر) تربط كل الفصول، تبدأ أشكالاً مسطحة وتصبح أجساماً ثم حبراً سائلاً ثم ملايين الجسيمات. الخلفية تتبدل بين أسود وكريمي، والإطار التقني ثابت.

## Concept
It builds up from simple to complex like a design lesson: point, then line, then plane, then volume, then fluid, then particles, then motion itself. The three Bauhaus primaries (blue, red, yellow) tie every chapter together, starting as flat shapes and becoming solids, then liquid ink, then countless particles. Backgrounds alternate between black and cream while the technical frame stays fixed.

## Motion rules
- Escalating complexity: each chapter (~1.5-2.5 s) uses a more advanced technique than the last; hard cuts on the beat between them.
- The three primaries carry through every technique: blue #2b3fe0, red #e8322c, yellow #f5c518, on black #0b0b0d or cream #efebe2.
- Fixed HUD: small logo of three shapes and reel label top-left, timecode top-right, chapter number and name bottom-left, a technical spec (grid size, particle count, field of view) bottom-right in tiny monospace.
- Type: heavy wide grotesk caps for chapter words, outlined or glassy variants when the word sits over simulation; monospace for HUD.
- Simulation chapters (fluid, particles) run in WebGL shaders; the 3D chapter uses physically based glossy and chrome materials with a reflective floor.
- A short rewind flicker before the end card and an iris close as the final out.
