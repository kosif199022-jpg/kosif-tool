## الفكرة
الفكرة تحويل سبعين عاماً من السياسة إلى لعبة ورق واضحة: معسكران (القصر والحركة الوطنية) لكلٍّ منهما رمز ولون، وكل حزب بطاقة، وكل زعيم بطاقة بصورة ظلية بلا ملامح بدلاً من صور حقيقية. يتقدم الفيلم زمنياً من الاستقلال إلى اليوم، ويقطع بين البطاقات بلوحات عناوين لمراحل الصراع، ولحظات أحداث برسم مسطح بسيط، ثم أرقام الانتخابات الأخيرة، ليصل إلى سؤال مفتوح للمشاهد. كل النص عربي بخط عرض عريض مستدير والتفاصيل بخط نصي واضح.

## Concept
The idea turns seventy years of politics into a clear card game: two camps (the palace and the national movement), each with a symbol and colour, every party a card and every leader a card with a faceless flat silhouette instead of real photos. The film moves chronologically from independence to today, cutting between cards with chapter panels for each phase of the struggle, simple flat illustrations for key events, then recent election numbers, arriving at an open question for the viewer. All text is Arabic, a heavy rounded display face for headlines and a clean text face for details.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-4 s: Three one-line hooks, each a full-colour panel (navy, sand, dark green) with a huge Arabic word in gold or cream, pushed off by the next panel sliding in from the side.
- 4-7 s: Split screen: two columns (palace camp with a crown icon in olive-brown, national movement with a blood-drop icon in crimson) list the parties; a round crown badge sits on the dividing navy strip.
- 7-60 s: Party and figure cards alternate: a party card has a big outlined year top-left, a heavy party name, leader and one-line note, on a diagonal-stripe background in the party colour with a camp tag top-right; a figure card shows a cream silhouette (fez, sunglasses or tie variants) in a framed coloured box beside name, role and dates.
- 43-70 s: Chapter panels with a centred title and subtitle on deep maroon or slate; a split panel shows the palace reply on one side and the opposition on the other with a speech-bubble quote and a small cut-out portrait.
- 70-100 s: Event vignettes in flat illustration: a palace colonnade with silhouetted figures and a star burst; a white airliner chased by dark fighter jets across a pale blue sky; a desert of dunes crossed by a long line of tiny marching figures; each with a red date headline and a two-line caption bottom-right.
- 100-175 s: Later decades continue with cards (the same template recoloured), a big 'N vs M' seats headline, a phone mock-up of a chat with a hashtag for a youth movement, and single-word or single-year slams on solid red or cream.
- 175-211 s: Data and close: a turnout percentage in red over a small bar chart by year; a seat-majority line; then a final question in cream on near-black with a tiny footer credit.

## Motion rules
- Arabic-first, RTL: headlines in a heavy rounded Arabic display face (Lalezar-like) never broken across joined letters; details in a clean Arabic text sans; Western digits for years, set large and outlined/dim when used as a background year.
- Palette is flat and saturated, one solid colour per panel: navy #1b2340, gold #e8b84a, crimson #7a1420, bright red #d4202c, forest green #12402e, sand #d4aa70, purple #4a2a6a, teal #1e5a5a, cream #f6efe0; text is cream or gold, never pure white.
- Transitions: the next panel pushes the current one sideways (hard-edged horizontal slide ~12 frames, ease-out) so the timeline always feels like it is moving; occasional direct cuts on single-word slams.
- Text builds top to bottom inside each card: tag pill, small name line, heavy name, role in gold, one-line note; each line fades/rises ~8 px with a 4-frame stagger.
- People are never photographed in cards: a cream bust silhouette with one identifying prop (fez, sunglasses, red tie) inside a thick cream frame on the party colour.
- Party cards use a slow diagonal-stripe drift in the background (~10 px/s) and keep a camp tag with icon (crown or drop) pinned top-right; holds of 4-9 s per card to match narration.
