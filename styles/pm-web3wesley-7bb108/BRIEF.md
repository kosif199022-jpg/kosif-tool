## الفكرة
الفكرة سيرة ذاتية تتحرك: البطاقة تعد بأربع مهارات (النوع، الشكل، الفضاء، الزمن)، وكل فصل يثبت مهارة بعرض قصير في وسيطها الخاص: تايبوغرافي، أشكال ثنائية الأبعاد، مشهد ثلاثي الأبعاد، ومحاكاة جسيمات. لوحة باوهاوس محدودة (برتقالي، أسود، كريمي، أصفر، أزرق) تربط كل شيء، وطبقة واجهة تقنية رفيعة (توقيت، رقم الفصل، اسم منحنى التسارع) في الزوايا تجعلها تبدو كأداة مصمم.

## Concept
The idea is a résumé that moves: the card promises four skills (type, form, space, time), and each chapter proves one with a short demo in its own medium: typography, 2D shapes, a 3D scene and a particle simulation. A tight Bauhaus palette (orange, black, cream, yellow, blue) ties it together, and a thin technical HUD in the corners (timecode, chapter number, easing curve name) makes it feel like a designer's tool.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.7s: A blurred cream card slides in on black and settles: an editorial résumé with a big serif name and orange dot, role line, an italic one-liner and a ruled list of four skills.
- 1.7-4.3s: Hard cut to orange: 'TYPE' in giant black grotesk on a baseline grid with font metrics bottom-left; it squashes wider, then turns into a cream italic serif 'type' with a stacked black extrusion and a rotating circular badge.
- 4.3-7.3s: Cream background: 'FORM' with an orange circle for the O; letters motion-blur apart and the frame fills with a grid of quarter circles, squares and triangles in orange, black, yellow and blue that flip and rotate cell by cell.
- 7.3-10s: A 3D isometric field of extruded blocks and cylinders in the same colours with 'SPACE' lying on the floor; a red sphere drops and bounces among them on a parabola.
- 10-12.9s: An orange flash, then black: thousands of thin particle strokes in a ball form the word 'TIME' inside a clock-tick ring, then spiral into a vortex and concentric orange rings.
- 12.9-15s: The résumé card returns; the skills list gains small icons for each chapter and an 'available for work' line appears.

## Motion rules
- Palette: signal orange #fe491b, black #0a0a0a, cream #f3eee6, yellow #f2b632, blue #2346e0.
- Type: giant condensed black grotesk for chapter words, a high-contrast serif (upright and italic) for the name and the 'type' morph, tiny monospace for HUD labels.
- A persistent HUD frame: corner crop marks, timecode top-right, chapter label bottom-left, the current easing name bottom-right, and a ruler tick bar along the bottom.
- Each chapter is about 2.5-3 s and switches with a hard cut or a full-frame colour flash; inside a chapter, motions are snappy with expo and back eases and visible motion blur.
- The shape grid animates cell by cell with staggered 90-degree flips, never all at once.
- Each chapter changes medium (flat type, 2D shapes, lit 3D, particles) while the palette stays fixed, so the reel feels varied but unified.
- Bookend: the opening card returns at the end with small additions, closing the loop.
