## الفكرة
الريل درس صغير مقنّع: كل مشهد يطبق مبدأً من مبادئ التحريك ثم يسميه بملاحظة مكتوبة بخط اليد وسهم منحنٍ، كأن المصمم يعلّق على عمله. الكرة الزرقاء هي البطل الذي يتكرر (في الارتداد، في نقطة العنوان، في مركز الحلقة، ونقطة الاسم الأخير). خط زمني رفيع أسفل الشاشة يسمي المبدأ الحالي ويتقدم.

## Concept
The reel is a disguised mini lesson: each scene applies one animation principle and then names it with a handwritten note and a curved arrow, as if the designer were annotating their own work. The blue ball is the recurring hero (in the bounce, as the headline's full stop, at the centre of the loader and as the final dot). A thin timeline along the bottom names the current principle and advances.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Light grey studio gradient with a black floor line: a blue ball drops and bounces with onion-skin ghost circles, squashing on impact; handwritten notes (slow in/out in blue, squash in red, stretch) write on with little arrows.
- 1.8-3.5s: A heavy condensed black headline assembles letter by letter with tumbling, blurred letters landing in place; the ball rolls in as the full stop, a blue marker underline draws, and notes about stagger and overlap appear.
- 3.7-5.5s: Dark slate frames, one word each: a white word stretching with vertical motion smear, a word spinning letters on their axis, then a red word that hard-snaps into place on red guide lines with a 'no ease, on purpose' note.
- 5.6-7.8s: Back to light grey: a position-over-time graph draws a straight line while a square jerks along a track with red ghost frames ('linear = robotic'), then the note gets struck through and the line morphs into an S-curve, the ghosts bunch at the ends ('ease in-out', 'spacing!').
- 8-10.8s: A wipe to dark slate with a faint dot grid: a blue comet of particles swoops in arcs, red sparks trailing behind, the grid warps as it passes ('follow-through'); the plane tilts in perspective.
- 11-12.6s: A circular loader: ring of small icons around a blue dot, a white arc filling with a percentage counting 26 → 100, then the dot squashes flat before release ('anticipation').
- 13-15s: Light grey end card: two lines of heavy condensed type tumble in and settle into a role line over the large name with the blue ball as the full stop, plus a handwritten 'open to new projects' note with an arrow.

## Motion rules
- Each principle gets ~1.5-2.5 s and is demonstrated before it is labelled; labels are handwritten script notes that write on with a hand-drawn curved arrow.
- Annotation colour code: blue #2f6fe4 for the correct technique, red #e8334a for warnings, impact moments and 'wrong' notes that later get struck through.
- Show motion physics explicitly: onion-skin ghosts, squash and stretch on the ball, motion smears on words, bunching ghost frames on eased tracks.
- Alternate light grey #ececec studio frames with dark slate #23262d frames; transitions are hard cuts or a soft horizontal wipe.
- A thin bottom timeline with tick marks shows the current principle's name and a moving playhead throughout.
- Type: an extra-bold condensed sans (black on light, white on dark) for words, a casual handwriting font for notes, tiny mono for timecode top-right.
