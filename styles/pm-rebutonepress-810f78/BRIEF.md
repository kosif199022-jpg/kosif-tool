## الفكرة
الفكرة شرح تمدد الزمن عند أفق الحدث كقصة قصيرة هادئة: سفينة أم ترسل مسباراً إلى الداخل، ونرى من جهتها كيف يحمرّ ضوؤه ويبطؤ وكأنه يتجمد، ثم نرى من جهة المسبار أن ثوانيه ما زالت ثواني عادية. الصورة واحدة تقريباً طوال الوقت (الثقب الأسود وقرصه)، والمعنى يأتي من الأرقام المتغيرة في اللوحتين ومن الترجمة. الإيقاع بطيء جداً ومهيب.

## Concept
The idea is to explain time dilation at the event horizon as a quiet short story: a mothership sends a probe inward, and from the ship's side we see its light redden and slow as if frozen, then from the probe's side we see that its seconds are still ordinary seconds. The image is almost one thing throughout (the black hole and its disk), and the meaning comes from the changing numbers in the two panels and the subtitles. The pacing is very slow and solemn.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-25s: Letterboxed starfield; a tiny lensed black hole with a bright disk sits centre and slowly grows; no text at first.
- 25-100s: Slow push-in: the disk tilts, the lensed ring above and below the shadow becomes clear and the starfield warps around it; one subtitle line per idea about bent light, mass and size, light-crossing time and disk speed.
- 100-150s: Close framing where the white-gold disk and the dark shadow fill the frame; corner panels appear: mothership top-left (local time, a ratio), probe top-right (elapsed time, redshift z, signal in dB); a subtitle about releasing a probe that blinks every second.
- 150-215s: The probe is a small glowing dot drifting toward the shadow; its panel numbers climb (redshift rising, signal falling to about -42 dB) while the dot dims; subtitles say its light reddens and its time seems to stop as seen from the ship.
- 215-260s: Hard switch to the probe's viewpoint: the disk swings across the frame at a new angle with a looser starfield; the panel layout flips (probe left, ship right with a speed ratio); subtitles say time runs normally on board and the ship's clock looks slower.
- 260-285s: The horizon has no marker: the frame nearly fades to black, then returns to the ship's view where the probe panel reads lost; a subtitle about the last light never reaching the ship.
- 285-300s: Dim veil over the disk and a centred two-character title in white with a tiny subtitle line below.
