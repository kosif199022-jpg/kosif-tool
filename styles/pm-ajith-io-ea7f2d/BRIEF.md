## الفكرة
الموضوع أداة تحوّل المستند إلى فيديو مرسوم، والفيلم يروي ذلك كرحلة: من ورقة بسيطة على سطح ورقي إلى عالم ملوّن من البطاقات والحروف والضوء ثم العودة إلى الورقة نفسها وقد صارت فيديو. لا شبكة ولا أرقام؛ الحركة كلها انتقالات متصلة: تقريب يخترق المستند، مسح ألوان، طيران بين البطاقات في عمق ثلاثي الأبعاد، وسرب يتحرك ككائن حي.

## Concept
The subject is a tool that turns a document into an illustrated video, and the film tells it as a journey: from a plain sheet on a paper surface into a colourful world of cards, letters and light, then back to the same sheet now ready to become a video. There is no grid and no numbers; all motion is continuous transitions: a zoom that punches through the document, colour wipes, a 3D fly-through of cards and a flock that moves like a living thing.

## Motion rules
- No hard cuts: zoom-throughs, colour wipes and morphs carry every change of world.
- Bookend the film on the same paper set (document, flower, horizon) so the journey reads as a loop.
- Depth is real: cards fly through a 3D space and the wall tilts in perspective; the letter flock uses a boids-style simulation.
- Light is a drawn element: the orb has concentric rings and becomes a stroke drawn along a path with a warm glow.
- Medium-fast pacing with energy peaks around 4-7 s, 17-19 s and 25-26 s.
- Palette: paper #e6e5d7, navy #17113b, purple #6a427f, rose #b17590, orange #e8623a and gold #f7c06a, plus full rainbow stripes for the wipe.
- Type is minimal: a handwritten script for the slogan (write-on) and a geometric sans 700 for the logo.
