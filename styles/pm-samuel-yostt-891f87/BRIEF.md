## الفكرة
الإعلان يشرح الخدمة في سبع جمل قصيرة، جملة لكل مشهد، والنص دائماً يساراً أو في المنتصف بخط بلا زوائد ثقيل، والصور والهواتف تملأ اليمين. الحركة سلسة وسريعة بضبابية حركة كأنها واجهة تطبيق حديثة، والبرتقالي يظهر فقط حيث يجب أن تنظر.

## Concept
The promo explains the service in seven short sentences, one per scene, with copy always on the left or centred in a heavy sans and photos and phones filling the right. Motion is smooth and fast with motion blur like a modern app UI, and orange appears only where you should look.

## Motion rules
- Background: white #ffffff with a large blurred peach glow #f9c9a8 at the bottom edge that drifts; cards white with soft grey shadows.
- One accent: orange #f0652a for the dot, selection border, chart line and CTA; success green #2fbf71 and error red #e5484d appear only in the winner pick.
- Type: heavy tight grotesk in near-black #111111, left-aligned two-line statements; secondary words in mid-grey #9a9a9a (two-tone taglines).
- Very fast: one statement every 1.5-2 s; transitions are motion-blurred pushes, vertical slot rolls and blur-to-focus (about 250-400 ms, strong ease-out).
- Photo and phone content always moves (slot spins, fan-outs, diagonal mosaic scroll) while copy stays still once landed.
- Numbers roll up and the chart draws in about 600 ms before the selection beat.
