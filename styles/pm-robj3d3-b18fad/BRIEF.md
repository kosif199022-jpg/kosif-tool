## الفكرة
التيزر يلعب على معنى حرف X: الجمهور يظن أنه يعني منصة واحدة، والفيلم يفكك هذا الافتراض خطوة بخطوة ثم يعد بمعنى أوسع (أداة متعددة الحسابات أو المحتوى). كل شيء داكن ومنخفض الإضاءة، والضوء الوحيد هو حافة نحاسية حمراء متوهجة كأنها سلك ساخن، مما يعطي إحساس «سر يُكشف». الإيقاع بطيء في البداية وسريع في التبديلات ثم يهدأ في الختام.

## Concept
The teaser plays on what the letter X means: the audience assumes it means one platform, and the film takes that assumption apart step by step before promising a broader meaning (a tool for many accounts or posts). Everything is dark and low-key, and the only light is a glowing copper-red rim like a hot wire, giving a 'secret being revealed' feel. Pacing is slow at the start, fast in the swaps, then calm at the close.

- 6.2-7.4 ث: يبقى «X = ؟» فقط، وعلامة الاستفهام بسيريف مائل رفيع؛ ويحتفظ X بتوهج أحمر.

- 12.5-15 ث: رمز لهب واسم المنتج بخط عريض مع لمعة تمر عليه، ثم سطر أبيض بالادعاء، و«قريباً.» بسيريف مائل، ورابط صغير جداً، فوق تظليل دافئ.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-2.5s: Extreme close-up of a huge dark X: thin copper-red light streaks slide along its bevelled edges with a warm bloom, camera drifting slowly.
- 2.5-3.5s: Pull back to a small dark X at centre; its edges ignite in red, a tiny grey line types below it.
- 3.5-4.8s: A bright white X, an equals sign and the old platform name in a bold sans; the subline finishes.
- 4.8-6.2s: A red line strikes through the name; replacement words flicker in and out with blur and glitch (a ticker symbol, a cross-out, a dash).
- 6.2-7.4s: Only 'X = ?' remains, the question mark in a thin italic serif; the X keeps a red glow.
- 7.4-10s: A single dark post card with a red outline sits inside a faint X; it multiplies into columns of cards (text posts, video cards) that fill the frame in depth and drift.
- 10-12s: Cards dissolve into scattered small X particles, grey and red, that link with faint lines into a constellation, then collapse into one glowing X.
- 12.5-15s: A flame emoji and a bold product name with a sparkle sweep, then a white claim line, an italic serif 'Soon.' and a tiny URL, over a warm vignette.

## Motion rules
- Near-black world: background #0b0908 with a warm radial glow #2a120c behind the focus; nothing is ever brighter than the white headline #f2f0ee.
- Single accent: copper-red rim light #e0552f to #ff7a4a with additive bloom, used for edge streaks, strike-throughs, card outlines and some particles.
- Type: bold geometric sans for the equation, a thin italic serif for '?' and 'Soon.', tiny grey #8a8580 sans sublines.
- Light streaks travel along edges at ~1.5 s per pass with a bright head and fading tail; slow camera dolly in the opening (~5% scale over 2.5 s).
- Word swaps are 120-200 ms with motion blur, chromatic offset and opacity flicker; the strike line draws left to right in ~250 ms.
- Card wall: one element duplicates outward in staggered waves (~60 ms per card) with depth blur on far cards, all drifting slowly.
- Particle constellation: seeded X glyph particles with proximity lines that fade in and out, then converge to the centre in ~600 ms.
