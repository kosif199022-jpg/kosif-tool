## الفكرة
الفكرة رحلة زمنية بلا قطع: المكان نفسه يُرى من الغروب حتى الليل، ومن الحاضر (أبراج زجاجية) إلى الماضي (الجناح التاريخي على الضفة). الكاميرا ثابتة تقريباً والتغيير كله في الضوء ولون السماء والعناصر التي تظهر وتختفي على خط الأفق. الشعر الكلاسيكي هو السرد، يُكتب في أعمدة رأسية رفيعة بخط سيريف رمادي فاتح كأنه نقش على لوحة، مع ختم أحمر صغير في النهاية.

## Concept
The idea is a cut-free journey through time: the same place is seen from sunset to night, and from the present (glass towers) to the past (the historic pavilion on the bank). The camera is almost locked and all the change lives in the light, the sky colour and what appears or disappears on the horizon line. Classical verse is the narration, written in thin vertical columns in a pale grey serif like an inscription on a scroll painting, with a small red seal at the end.

- 24-29 ث: يُكتب بيت أخير أعلى اليسار، ويظهر عمود تعليق صغير وختم أحمر على اليمين؛ ثم يخفت الكادر كله إلى ما يقارب السواد.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1s: Fade up from a dark sepia veil onto a still river, a modern skyline silhouette on the far bank and a low sun at the horizon.
- 1-5s: Pink-orange sky with streaky cloud bands; a two-column verse writes on top-left, one character at a time from the top of each column, as a single bird crosses.
- 5-10s: Second verse column writes on at the right edge while the sun sinks and its glitter path on the water dims.
- 10-16s: The skyline fades out and the horizon flattens; clouds morph into marbled shapes and the sky shifts to violet-blue; a third verse column writes on at the left.
- 16-20s: A low tree line returns and the red-and-dark-roofed pavilion complex slides in from the right edge, settling centre; a full moon rises beside it.
- 20-24s: Night: the pavilion's tiers light up warm gold, its reflection streaks the water and small lantern lights appear and bob on the river.
- 24-29s: A last couplet writes on top-left, a small caption column and red seal appear at the right; the whole frame then dims to near black.

- Scene elements enter slowly: the skyline cross-fades out over about 2 s, the pavilion slides in from the right over about 2 s with an ease-out, the moon rises over about 3 s.
