## الفكرة
الفكرة نكتة بسيطة تُعامل بجدية تقنية: سؤال «ما يوم اليوم؟» يُعالَج كأنه استعلام في نظام، والنظام «يمسح» التقويم على إيقاع الموسيقى قبل أن يعلن الجواب بانفجار تايبوغرافي. كل شيء يُعد على شبكة بارات ونبضات (الإطار يعرض رقم البار والنبضة)، فكل تغيير يقع على نبضة. لونان فقط مع الكريمي: فحمي وبرتقالي محمر.

## Concept
The idea is a simple joke treated with technical seriousness: the question 'what day is it?' is handled like a system query, and the system 'scans' the calendar to the music's beat before announcing the answer with a type explosion. Everything is counted on a grid of bars and beats (the frame shows the bar and beat number), so every change lands on a beat. Two colours plus cream: charcoal and a red-orange.

## Motion rules
- Everything snaps to a 120 BPM grid (500 ms beats); the HUD footer shows bar and beat counters and a small progress tick.
- Palette: charcoal #141210 with a soft radial orange glow #3a1a10 at centre, cream #efe9df for type, red-orange #ff4a1c for accents, mid grey #5a5550 for dimmed elements.
- Type: heavy condensed sans uppercase for English (FRIDAY, WEEK 40, tile labels), bold Japanese gothic for the question and answer, tiny tracked monospace for HUD labels.
- Typing: one character per ~120 ms with a block orange caret that blinks at 2 Hz while idle.
- Selection box hops instantly on the beat with a 100 ms glow flare; the lock state dims all other tiles to about 35% opacity.
- Hero slam: scale from about 130% to 100% in 150 ms with a 3-frame RGB split (red and cyan offsets) then a solid orange extruded shadow; background marquee rows scroll slowly at low opacity.
- Data rows slide up 20 px and fade in on successive beats; counters roll with blur on changing digits; progress bar fills linearly.
