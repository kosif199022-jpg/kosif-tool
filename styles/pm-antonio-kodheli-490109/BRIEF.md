## الفكرة
الموضوع موقع يجمع فيديوهات موشن مع النصوص التي صنعتها، والإعلان يثبت ذلك بالتجول داخل الواجهة الحقيقية بدل وصفها. الكاميرا هي الراوي: تتصغر وتقترب وتنزلق، ومؤشر الفأرة يقود القصة من التصفح إلى فتح عنصر إلى نسخ نصه. الألوان هادئة (أبيض دافئ وأسود) والأحمر والأزرق يأتيان من محتوى البطاقات نفسها.

## Concept
The subject is a site that collects motion videos together with the text that produced them, and the promo proves it by touring the real interface instead of describing it. The camera is the narrator: it pulls back, pushes in and glides, while the mouse cursor drives the story from browsing to opening an item to copying its text. The palette is calm (warm white and black) and the red and blue come from the card content itself.

## Proposed scenes

## Motion rules
- One continuous virtual camera over a flat UI: scale and translate with long ease-in-out moves (about 1-2 s each), never rotation.
- Section changes are blur transitions: a quick directional motion blur or a full gaussian blur-out and blur-in of about 0.3-0.5 s instead of hard cuts.
- A visible arrow cursor drives actions; each click shows a red halo dot (#e8402f) and the target reacts (card opens, button state flips).
- Text emphasis is a red highlight box behind a phrase, drawn in about 200 ms after the camera settles.
- Palette: warm off-white #f2f1ee canvas, near-black #111111 type and pills, white #ffffff cards with soft shadows, accent red #e8402f and electric blue #2b3ae0.
- Type: tight bold grotesk for headlines, regular grotesk for subtitles, monospace for the text block; everything left-aligned and generous in whitespace.
