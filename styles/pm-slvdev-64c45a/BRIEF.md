## الفكرة
الإعلان يجيب عن سؤال واحد: ماذا يمكن أن تصنع؟ والجواب قائمة سريعة، لكل عنصر عالم صغير بلونه وشكله: هاتف بإشعارات، أوراق قصة، رسم بياني ينمو، لعبة تتكسر قطعها، موقع يُنقر، كود يُصلح. الإيقاع لعوب ومسرع، والانتقال بين العوالم بمسحات ألوان ضخمة، والختام يجمع كل العوالم كبطاقات حول الرسالة الأخيرة.

## Concept
The spot answers one question: what can you make? The answer is a fast list, each item a small world with its own colour and object: a phone with notifications, story pages, a growing chart, a breaking block game, a clickable website, a code fix. The rhythm is playful and quick, worlds change with huge colour wipes, and the ending gathers all the worlds as cards around the final message.

## Motion rules
- One colour world per item, about 1.5 s each: blue #2f4fd8, yellow #f2cf4a, green #2e6b4a, orange #d9603b, pink #f1b3ad, charcoal #1c1a17; transitions are full-frame colour wipes, some diagonal, in about 250 ms.
- Bouncy, springy motion: objects drop or slide in with overshoot (spring, light damping) and settle; small shapes keep drifting slowly in the background.
- Each world has one hero object that does one action: notifications pop, pages stack, bars grow, blocks shatter, a window rotates flat, a red line turns green.
- Type mix on purpose: heavy tight grotesk for statements, italic serif for literary items, monospace for code, and many mixed faces in the word burst.
- Closing structure: a giant two-word line, then the frame zooms out to a mosaic of all previous worlds as cards on a dark ground before the cream end card #f3eee4.
- Fast cadence of about 10 distinct beats in 18 s; the end card holds about 3 s with the cursor nudging the button.
