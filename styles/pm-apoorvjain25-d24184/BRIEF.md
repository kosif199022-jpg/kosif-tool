## الفكرة
الفكرة مقارنة صادمة بين زمنين: الفوتون يقضي آلاف السنين محبوساً يرتد داخل الشمس، ثم يقطع المسافة إلى الأرض في ثماني دقائق فقط، لينتهي في عينك في جزء من ثانية لم تنتبه له. الإيقاع نفسه يجسد ذلك: نصف أول بطيء ومزدحم ومحبط، ونصف ثانٍ منطلق كالسهم، ونهاية صامتة على الأسود.

## Concept
The idea is a jolting contrast of two clocks: a photon spends many thousands of years trapped, bouncing inside the Sun, then crosses to Earth in about eight minutes, only to land in your eye in a moment you never noticed. The pacing embodies it: a slow, crowded, frustrating first half, a second half that shoots forward like an arrow, and a quiet ending on black.

- 6.2-7.5 ث: قطع إلى الأسود: الشمس قرص برتقالي كامل عليه المسار المتشابك، تحت «170,000 سنة»؛ ثم كلمة «حر» وحدها مع خروج الفوتون.

- 12.5-14.5 ث: ومضة بيضاء عند الوصول ثم صورة جوية ضبابية للأرض والسحب؛ عداد ارتفاع يتناقص و«تقريباً» بخط أبيض ضخم على سماء زرقاء.
- 14.5-17 ث: عين بشرية مرسومة عن قرب بالرموش؛ علامة تصويب وتسميات صغيرة تثبت على الحدقة؛ و«أنت» تغطي القزحية، ثم تدخل الكاميرا في الحدقة وتنغلق علامة تصويب حمراء.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.5s: Deep orange-brown core with soft out-of-focus particles; a small tracked caption types out and a temperature number starts climbing; a white-hot photon flares in.
- 1.5-2.5s: A big white condensed 'WRONG WAY.' slams in with RGB split; the HUD top-left shows years trapped, depth, temperature and bounces.
- 2.5-6.2s: The photon draws a zigzag trail of thin white segments, bouncing with small ring flashes; the word 'AGAIN' stamps in at random angles, multiplying until the screen is full; counters race and the particle field brightens to gold.
- 6.2-7.5s: Cut to black: the Sun as a full orange disc with the tangled path on it, under '170,000 YEARS.'; then a single 'FREE.' as the photon escapes.
- 7.5-12.5s: Navy space with streaking star lines; a clock T+ counts up top-left, distance and speed top-right, a Sun-to-Earth progress bar along the bottom; huge translucent grey words pass behind the photon one per beat while Mercury and Venus slide by with labels.
- 12.5-14.5s: White flash on arrival, then a blurred aerial Earth with clouds; an altitude counter drops and a big white 'ALMOST.' sits on blue sky.
- 14.5-17s: An illustrated close-up human eye with lashes; a targeting reticle and tiny HUD labels lock on the pupil; 'YOU.' covers the iris, then the camera pushes into the pupil and a red reticle closes.
- 17-20s: Black: a serif italic line about how long it took to reach you types on, then 'YOU BLINKED.' lands below in big white condensed type with a slight RGB fringe; hold.

## Motion rules
- Three colour worlds: solar core #3a1308 to #c76a1e with gold bokeh #f2b05a; space navy #0d0b26 with white streaks; final black #000000 with white type.
- Headline words are heavy condensed uppercase sans in white #ffffff with a 2-3 px red/cyan chromatic offset that settles on impact.
- The HUD is small monospace in white at 70% opacity; numbers update every frame so the counters feel live.
- Phase one is chaotic and accelerating: the zigzag path draws in short straight segments, and 'AGAIN' stamps arrive faster and faster at random rotations in rust #a8402a.
- Phase two is linear and fast: star streaks radiate from centre, giant background words swap about every 0.8 s, and planets parallax past.
- Transitions are hard cuts or white flashes at the phase changes (escape, arrival, eye).
- The ending slows right down: a typed serif italic line, a beat, then the punchline drops and holds for about 2 s.
