## الفكرة
الفكرة كشف الحقيقة بالأرقام: العميل دفع ثمن قائمة روابط، والفيلم يفتح الفاتورة أمامه رقماً بعد رقم بهدوء بارد. لا مؤثرات، فقط خط كبير واضح وأحمر واحد يُستخدم للخسارة. كل فصل مرقم برقم صغير ويقدم دليلاً واحداً، والنبرة تتصاعد من السؤال إلى الصدمة إلى الحل الرخيص.

## Concept
The idea is exposing the truth with numbers: the client paid for a list of links, and the film opens the invoice in front of them, figure after figure, with cold calm. No effects, just big clear type and a single red used for loss. Each chapter has a small number and offers one piece of evidence, and the tone climbs from question to shock to a cheap fix.

## Motion rules
- Palette: black #141414, white #ffffff, off-white #f6f5f2, loss red #b8474a, dot pink #e98a86, light grey dots #d9d9d9, caption grey #9a9a9a.
- Type: one neutral grotesk in bold for headlines and numbers; tiny tracked monospace for chapter kickers and footnotes; the logo is a red brush script.
- Headlines type on per word with a masked rise; there is no bounce, everything eases out in 300-500 ms.
- Numbers count up with tabular figures; percentages and currency roll digits rather than fading.
- Dot matrices fill in a scan order (row by row) at about 10 ms per dot; red means bad, black marks clusters.
- Scene changes are hard cuts between black and white for emphasis, and quick fades between chapters; a thin progress line runs along the bottom.
- One idea per screen with lots of empty space; each chapter holds its final state for about 1 s.
