## الفكرة
الريل مبني على إيقاع موسيقي ثابت (يظهر رقم الإيقاع في الواجهة) وكل فصل يقدّم مهارة في نحو ثانيتين: خطوط حركية، أنماط هندسية، جسيمات، ثلاثي أبعاد، محاكاة، تصميم، صوت. النقطة الحمراء هي الخيط الرابط: تبدأ الفيلم، وتصير خطاً، وتعود في النهاية كنقطة بعد الاسم. الخلفية تتنقل بين أسود دخاني وكريمي ولوني الأحمر والأزرق.

## Concept
The reel rides a steady musical tempo (the BPM is shown in the HUD) and each chapter presents one skill in about two seconds: kinetic type, geometric pattern, particles, 3D, simulation, design, sound. A red dot is the through-line: it opens the film, becomes a line, and returns at the end as the full stop after the name. Grounds move between smoky black, cream and the signature red and blue.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.9s: Layout: a red dot pulses on a dark ground, stretches into a glowing horizontal line, and a fine grid with a small arc gauge draws in.
- 1.9-3.8s: Kinetic type: a heavy black word on cream; a red first letter attaches to make a new word, ghost rows slide past with motion blur, then outlined repeats fill the frame while a small 3D object rotates inside one letter.
- 3.8-5.6s: Pattern: a flat Bauhaus mosaic of squares with circles, rings, quarter-circles, leaves and stripes in red, blue, yellow, black and cream; tiles flip and swap on the beat and the whole grid tilts away.
- 5.6-7.5s: Particles: warm golden particles swirl into a ring, then a twisting ribbon, then settle into a dotted sphere.
- 7.5-9.4s: 3D and shading: a low-poly sphere with an orange-to-blue gradient and wireframe patch orbits; it shatters into triangles with a bright core, then reassembles into a faceted rock.
- 9.4-11.3s: Simulation: glossy metaballs with an orange-blue gradient stretch, split into pairs and shed small droplets that fall back and merge.
- 11.3-13s: Design / 3D / Sound: a bold word over a construction grid of circles and lines on red; an extruded white word on a blue perspective grid; a word over orange spectrum bars with reflections.
- 13-15s: Sign-off: the name in a heavy cream serif-ish sans on dark, a red dot drops in as the full stop with a ring pulse, a role line and an italic line about everything being generated in code.

## Motion rules
- Palette: smoky black #141416 with soft vignette, cream #ede8e0, red #d9442a, cobalt blue #2b3be0, yellow #f2c230, orange #ff8a2a; one ground per chapter.
- Every cut and tile swap lands on the beat of a constant tempo shown in the HUD; chapters last 2–4 bars (~1.9 s each).
- HUD on every frame: thin corner brackets, top-left 'name / reel year', top-right running timecode, bottom-left chapter number and name, bottom-right BPM, bar counter and four beat squares, plus a thin progress line.
- Type is a heavy geometric sans in caps for words; italic serif for the final line; tiny uppercase mono for HUD.
- Kinetic type uses horizontal slides with strong motion blur and outlined (stroke-only) repeats; the hero word stays sharp.
- 3D elements share one gradient material, orange #ff8a2a to blue #3a3fe0, glossy with soft studio light; transitions by shatter, scale-through or quick cross-fade.
- Red dot motif opens and closes the piece; on landing it emits one thin expanding ring.
