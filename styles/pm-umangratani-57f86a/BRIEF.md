## الفكرة
الشوريل يستعرض مهارات مصمم موشن مقطعاً مقطعاً، وكل مقطع مدته بار واحد تقريباً على إيقاع 128 نبضة. أربعة ألوان خلفية جريئة (أسود، أحمر، أزرق، أصفر، ثم كريمي) تتبدل مع كل مقطع، والنقطة الحمراء هي الخيط الذي يربطها من البداية حتى النقطة في آخر التوقيع.

## Concept
The reel shows off a motion designer's skills one segment at a time, each lasting roughly one bar at 128 BPM. Bold flat backgrounds (black, red, blue, yellow, then cream) switch with every segment, and a red dot is the thread that ties them, from the opening dot to the full stop in the final signature.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Near-black frame with thin HUD corners and tiny monospace readouts; a single red dot splits into two, then into a ring of eight that spins around a centre mark.
- 1.8-3.6s: Cut to a flat red slate: a heavy geometric sans word slams in, the next word arrives inside white bracket corners, and a second line rises with motion-blur smear.
- 3.6-5.6s: Back to black: a red circle inside a transform box with handles and a rotation readout morphs into a rotating blue squircle with ghost trails, then a red flower.
- 5.6-7.5s: Cream background covered in a dot grid; dots swell and turn red along travelling ripple rings, like a halftone wave.
- 7.5-9.4s: Electric blue: white particles form a sphere, unfold into a torus, then collapse into a wireframe cube with a yellow edge highlight that tumbles.
- 9.4-11.3s: Flat yellow: a thick ribbon striped red, white and blue with a black outline snakes in, ties a loop with little impact ticks, and exits right.
- 11.3-13s: Black: rows of tracked uppercase skill words scroll in alternating directions, then wrap into a rotating circular spiral around a red dot, which spins into concentric motion-blurred rings.
- 13-15s: Cream end card: a serif wordmark writes in while the red dot lands as its full stop, with a small tracked role line and a credits line beneath.

- واجهة محرر ثابتة: زوايا رفيعة على شكل L، وسم صغير أعلى اليسار، مؤشر بار ونبضات أعلى اليمين، وعداد إطارات أسفل اليمين بخط أحادي صغير.
- الخط: سانس هندسي ثقيل بأحرف كبيرة (شبيه Montserrat Black) للكلمات القوية مع ضبابية لطخة؛ وسانس متباعد بأحرف كبيرة لكلمات المهارات؛ وسيريف عالي التباين للتوقيع.

- النقطة الحمراء تتكرر كخيط رابط وتنتهي نقطةً في آخر التوقيع.

## Motion rules
- Palette: ink #121214, signal red #e8412f, electric blue #3d43f0, yolk yellow #f2c23a, cream #efe9df, white #ffffff; one flat background per segment.
- Segments change on the bar: hard cuts at about 1.9 s intervals (5.6, 7.5, 9.4, 11.3, 13.2 s), matching one bar at 128 BPM.
- Persistent editor HUD: thin L-corners, a tiny label top-left, a bar/BPM indicator top-right and a frame-counter timecode bottom-right in small monospace.
- Type: heavy geometric uppercase sans (Montserrat Black-like) for slams with smear blur; tracked uppercase sans for skill words; a high-contrast serif for the signature.
- Morphs use snappy overshoot easing (~250 ms in, settle with a small bounce); transforms show handles and live rotation numbers.
- The red dot recurs as the connective motif and ends as the period of the signature.
