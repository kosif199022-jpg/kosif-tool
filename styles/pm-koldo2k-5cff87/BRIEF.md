## الفكرة
فكرة سريالية على طريقة ماغريت: كل عالم يحتوي بوابة دائرية إلى العالم التالي، والكاميرا لا تتوقف عن الدفع للأمام. البوابات أشياء عتيقة دائرية (ساعة جيب، عدسة كاميرا، عدسة مكبرة) والأشياء الطافية قصاصات مطبوعة بحواف ورقية بيضاء وملمس حفر قديم، فوق مناظر طبيعية فوتوغرافية باهتة الألوان. لا قطع ولا نص، فقط حركة واحدة متصلة تعود إلى بدايتها.

## Concept
A Magritte-like surreal idea: every world contains a circular portal to the next, and the camera never stops pushing forward. The portals are round vintage objects (pocket watch, camera lens, magnifying glass), and the floating things are printed cut-outs with white paper borders and an old engraving texture, over muted photographic landscapes. No cuts and no text, just one continuous move that returns to its start.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.3s: World one: a muted blue sky with soft clouds over a green field and hazy blue mountains; a man in a white shirt walks away from camera; a large pocket watch hangs from the top and a black bowler hat drifts at right; the camera pushes steadily toward the watch.
- 1.3-2.5s: The watch fills the frame; its dial and numerals fade out as the glass becomes a window onto a rocky coast, with the brass bezel framing it.
- 2.5-4.6s: World two: the bezel passes the edges of frame; sea cliffs, a tide pool and calm water; a whale, an open umbrella, a box camera and a rotary phone float as white-bordered cut-outs, each drifting and turning slightly at its own speed.
- 4.6-6.7s: The box camera grows to dominate the frame; its textured body and chrome lens ring slide past as the push enters the lens.
- 6.7-8.5s: World three: through the lens glass, pale sand dunes under a hazy sky; an old key, a magnifying glass and a gramophone horn float and tumble slowly.
- 8.5-10s: The magnifying glass rises toward centre and its lens shows a miniature of the opening field and sky, the frame matching world one so the clip can loop seamlessly.

## Motion rules
- One continuous exponential push-in for the whole clip: scale grows at a constant rate per second so the speed feels even across worlds; no cuts, no camera shake.
- Each portal is a circular mask on a round object; the next world is already visible inside it before the object fills the frame, then the object's rim slides off the edges.
- Floating objects are flat cut-outs with a 4-6 px white paper border, a subtle drop shadow and engraved halftone texture; each drifts and rotates a few degrees with its own slow parallax.
- Palette (estimated): sky blue #7f9cb8, cloud white #e8ebe6, field green #5f7a46, metal sepia #6b5d4c, deep brown #3a332c, sand #d8b48c; everything slightly desaturated.
- Finish: soft film grain, light vignette and a faint warm wash to unify photo and cut-out layers.
- Loop rule: the last frame's inner scene must equal the first frame at the matching scale so the end joins the start invisibly.
- No text and no UI; the clip relies entirely on imagery.
