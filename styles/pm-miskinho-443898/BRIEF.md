## الفكرة
خمس كلمات تحكي المنتج كله بالترتيب: فكرة واحدة، كل المنصات، مجدولة، منشورة، تتحسن. كل كلمة عنوان مرقم يجلس في الزاوية العليا اليسرى ويتبدل بحركة لفّ رأسية، وتحته يتحول نفس المحتوى (صورة منشور صيفي) من حالة إلى أخرى دون قطع: من فكرة إلى بطاقة إلى نسخ إلى خانات تقويم إلى نقاط على منحنى. الأبيض والأسود يحكمان، والأخضر يظهر فقط حين يأتي النجاح.

## Concept
Five words tell the whole product in order: one idea, every platform, scheduled, published, improving. Each is a numbered heading in the top-left slot that swaps with a vertical roll, and beneath it the same content (a summer-menu post photo) transforms from state to state without a cut: idea, card, copies, calendar slots, points on a curve. Black and white rule; green appears only when success arrives.

## Motion rules
- One persistent heading slot top-left: each new heading rolls up from below inside a mask while the old one exits upward, about 300 ms, with a small red index numeral above.
- Palette: white #ffffff, near-black #111111, success green #087850 with a pale area tint #e3f1ea, small red accent #d23b2f (estimated) for numerals and the top-post tag; photography supplies all other colour.
- Type: a heavy grotesk for headings ending in a full stop, a small neutral sans for UI labels.
- Continuity over cuts: the same post asset transforms through card, copies, calendar thumbnails and chart points; only one motion-blur whip at about 5.6 s.
- UI cards use soft long shadows and a slight perspective float; the fan-out is a staggered spread of about 80 ms per card.
- Status changes are staggered badge flips (tag text and colour change with a small scale pop) rather than full-screen changes.
- Pacing is even: roughly 2 s per heading, with the logo end card held about 3 s.
