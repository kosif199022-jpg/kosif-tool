## الفكرة
شوريل لمصمم موشن خيالي يستعرض أدواته بسرعة: تايبوغرافي، إيقاع، عمق، لون، زمن، فوضى ونظام. كل فصل بلون خلفية واحد صريح (أسود، أحمر، أبيض مكسور، أزرق كهربائي) وعناصر قليلة، ويربطها إطار «كاميرا» ثابت بمؤقت وعنوان الفصل، فتبدو كأنها شاشة مونتاج حية. الإيقاع يتصاعد حتى قطعات كل ربع ثانية عند الثانية 8.

## Concept
A showreel for a fictional motion designer that rushes through the toolkit: type, rhythm, depth, colour, time, chaos and order. Each chapter uses one flat field colour (black, red, off-white, electric blue) and few elements, tied together by a constant viewfinder overlay with timecode and chapter labels so it feels like a live edit monitor. The pace climbs to cuts every quarter second around 8 s.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-0.6s: Black frame with a thin white horizontal rule and tiny labels; viewfinder brackets and timecode already present.
- 0.6-1.8s: Red field: a heavy black greeting builds letter by letter with a masked wipe, a faint ring outline behind, a small underline and a kicker above.
- 1.8-3.5s: Off-white field: MOTION assembles; its O letters are red rings, a red construction grid of outlined letters flashes behind, a small tagline and vertical Japanese caption, then a blue dot drops into one ring.
- 3.5-5.6s: Electric blue grid of white dots with red and lime accents; dots morph to diamonds and crosses, then into rotating dashes forming a flow field, while an easing-curve graph widget plots in the corner.
- 5.6-8s: Black with navy glow: a spray of lines converges into a dotted 3D sphere with red nodes and an orbit ring, behind a giant outlined word; the sphere twists into a ribbon of particles.
- 8-10s: Quarter-second hard cuts: a huge cream kanji, a word with each letter a different colour between diagonal stripe bands, a word repeated in rows on red with motion blur.
- 10-11.8s: A navy particle vortex spirals, then streams into a flying swarm, with a two-line caption about order and chaos.
- 11.8-15s: Black: a cream condensed name pops in, a red underline draws, a tracked-out role line decodes letter by letter, three small coloured shapes appear above, then everything collapses to a single red dot.

## Motion rules
- Palette: black #0b0b0d, signal red #ef3a24, off-white #ece9e2, electric blue #2d3df0, lime #c8f03c, cream type #f3ead8, navy glow #1a2160.
- Type: ultra-heavy condensed grotesk caps for hero words, tiny tracked mono for HUD labels, and a CJK display face for the kanji beat.
- A viewfinder HUD stays on top throughout: corner brackets, a running timecode, chapter number and name, and a thin scrub bar along the bottom.
- Letters build with masked wipes and slight overshoot; grids morph shape-by-shape in a diagonal stagger (about 30 ms per cell); particles follow seeded flow fields.
- Cut rate accelerates: chapters of about 1.5-2.5 s, then cuts every 0.25 s between 8 and 10 s, then longer holds for the vortex and logo.
- Motion blur and slight chromatic fringing on fast moves; the end collapses to a single dot, mirroring a dot or line opening.
