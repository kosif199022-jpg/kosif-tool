## الفكرة
القصة رحلة واحدة من الرغبة إلى الطلب: مستخدم لا يعرف ماذا يريد، يكتب إحساسه، والتطبيق يفهمه ويقترح طبقاً ويطلبه له. الكاميرا قريبة جداً ومتحركة دائماً، تميل وتنزلق على الواجهة كأنها تقرأ مع المستخدم، والأبيض الواسع يجعل البرتقالي والليموني وصور الطعام الداكنة تقفز. الختام يهدأ فجأة إلى نص أسود صغير في منتصف فراغ أبيض.

## Concept
The story is one journey from craving to order: a user who does not know what they want types a feeling, and the app understands, suggests a dish and orders it. The camera is very close and always moving, tilting and sliding across the interface as if reading along, and the wide white lets the orange, lime and dark food photos pop. The ending suddenly calms into small black text in the middle of white space.

## Motion rules
- Camera is always in motion during the product part: tilts of 10-25 degrees, push-ins and slides with strong directional motion blur on fast moves; it goes still only for the closing lines.
- Palette: white #ffffff, near-black text #111111, orange bowl #f2703f, lime #caff70 for wipes and assistant bubbles, blue accent #3f5bea (estimated) for cursor bar and send button, iridescent pink-yellow-green edge glows.
- Type: a clean geometric sans throughout; product UI at real scale, hero words blown up to fill the frame in perspective; closing lines small and centred.
- Typing is character by character with a visible caret; the kinetic word pass lasts under one second.
- The loader is a flat-shape illustration: balls fall with gravity and squash slightly on contact with the bowl, which rocks on its stick.
- Transitions are wipes and slides, not crossfades: the lime field wipe, the carousel slide and the chat slide-up; one hard cut at about 7.3 s.
- Closing words fade from about 30% grey to full black in sequence, roughly 250 ms apart.
