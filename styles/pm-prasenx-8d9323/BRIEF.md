## الفكرة
الفكرة شوريل كل بكسل وكل صوت فيه مكتوب بالكود، فتأتي الصورة والإيقاع من مصدر واحد. النصف الأول عرض سريع لتقنيات الموشن (منحنيات، شيدر، جسيمات، نيون، فسيفساء، متساوي القياس)، والنصف الثاني «قطع على النبضة» صريح: كل كلمة لقطة كاملة بلون وتصميم مختلف، حتى الشعار.

## Concept
The idea is a showreel where every pixel and every sound is written in code, so image and rhythm come from one source. The first half is a quick tour of motion techniques (curves, shaders, particles, neon, mosaic, isometric); the second half is an explicit cut-on-the-beat sequence where each word is a full shot with its own colour and design, all the way to the logo.

## Proposed scenes

- 1.2-2.4s: A red square rotates in with heavy motion blur and speed lines, then a red field with a giant black condensed name split by a horizontal slice and a tracked white role line.
- 2.4-3.6s: A glossy black torus studded with neon lights spins on dark blue; cut to a mosaic grid of quarter-circles, rings and triangles in red, blue, black, white and lime flipping tile by tile.
- 3.6-5.6s: A dark tile grid fizzes into a word made of white fibres; a red, white and blue neon racetrack tunnel spirals toward the camera.
- 5.6-7.6s: A word filled with a moving blue-pink liquid texture on light grey; an isometric field of red-and-white cubes with lime tops rising and falling; a tilted wall of skill words in white and red scrolling past.

- 12.6-15s: End card on charcoal: red starburst mark and white wordmark, a tracked role line and a red year line; tiny credit text along the bottom.

## Motion rules
- Palette: charcoal #15161b, signal red #ef4a32, royal blue #3346e0, lime #d2f53c, off-white #ecebe6, white #ffffff, deep navy #10163a for 3D scenes.
- Type: tall condensed industrial sans (DIN-like) in caps for every hero word; small widely tracked caps for role lines; a monospace label for the bezier values.
- Two tempos: the first 7.6 s cut roughly every 0.6-1.3 s on technique changes; 7.6-12.8 s cuts land every 0.4 s (about 150 BPM), one word or flash per beat.
- Heavy motion blur, speed lines and glitch slices on transitions; shader textures (fibre, liquid) move continuously inside letterforms.
- 3D beats (torus, cube field, tunnel) are real-time renders with neon emissive accents and slow camera drift.
- The soundtrack is generated in code (Web Audio synthesis) and the beat grid drives every cut.
