## Concept
A premium science-channel documentary: one presenter talks to camera and explains superintelligence in plain language, and the film cuts to visual metaphors instead of charts (dominoes for a chain reaction, a gorilla for the species that is no longer the smartest, paper clips for a blind objective). Lighting is low and warm, and on-screen type is sparse and delicate: numbered chapter cards and dictionary-style definitions that type on letter by letter.
