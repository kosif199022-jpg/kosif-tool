## الفكرة
الفكرة أن كل ميزة جملة قصيرة من كلمتين أو ثلاث بخط سيريف أنيق، يرافقها رسم بياني مبني من نقاط برتقالية صغيرة: الملف، الرفع، طبقات البث، الكرة الأرضية، اللانهاية. النقطة هي وحدة البناء في الفيلم كله، فالأشكال تتجمع من نقاط وتتفكك إلى نقاط. البرتقالي هو اللون الوحيد، يتبدل موقعه بين الشكل والخلفية ليصنع إيقاعاً بصرياً.

## Concept
The idea is that each feature is a short two-to-three-word line in an elegant serif, paired with a graphic built from small orange dots: the file, the upload, the streaming layers, the globe, the infinity loop. The dot is the building unit of the whole film, so shapes assemble from dots and dissolve back into dots. Orange is the only colour, swapping between figure and ground to create a visual rhythm.

## Motion rules
- Dot-matrix language: every graphic is a grid or field of round orange dots that assemble, fill solid or disperse; transitions often go through a dot swirl.
- Palette: cream #f3f1ec, ink black #0d0b0a, signature orange #ea4c12, soft orange tint #f7d6c6, success green #2fbf5a; orange swaps between figure and ground.
- Type: a light high-contrast serif for headlines (sentence case with full stops), tiny tracked orange uppercase kickers above, monospace for file names and URLs; numbers set in the serif with orange units.
- Quick rhythm: a new feature about every 1.7-2 s; the ground colour flips (cream, black, orange) for hard contrast between beats.
- Headlines reveal with a vertical mask rising from the baseline over about 300 ms; counters roll with an ease-out.
- 3D-ish moments (tilted video planes, globe) use perspective with orange glow and fine grain; the rest is flat.
- Persistent frame chrome: tiny corner labels, a thin bottom progress rule with an orange segment marking the current section.
