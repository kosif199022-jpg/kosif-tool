## الفكرة
الفكرة أن «كل إطار قرار»: الريل يكشف أدوات صنعه بدل إخفائها. كل مشهد يحمل فوقه سطر تعليق كما في الكود يصف ما يحدث (المنحنى، عدد المثلثات، عدد الجسيمات، الشبكة)، ورقم الفصل واسمه أعلى اليمين، والخط الزمني الأحمر بعلامات الكيفريم يتقدم أسفل الشاشة على إيقاع 128 نبضة. لون واحد قوي (أحمر برتقالي) فوق الأسود والكريمي.

## Concept
The idea is that 'every frame is a decision': the reel exposes its own tools instead of hiding them. Each scene carries a code-style comment line describing what happens (the curve, the triangle count, the particle count, the grid), the chapter number and name top-right, and a red timeline with keyframe diamonds advancing along the bottom at 128 BPM. One strong colour (red-orange) over black and cream.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Keyframe: on near-black, a red diamond keyframe slides along a line, the line bends into an S-shaped easing curve with bezier handles and coefficients top-right, and the diamond grows as it reaches the top key.
- 1.8-3.5s: Kinetic type: a full red frame with a giant black word; a cream vignette frame with a blurred grey letter tumbling in; then a three-line statement in heavy cream and red type with stacked offset echo copies behind each line.
- 3.7-5.5s: Dimension: a faceted red octahedron spins inside an orbit ellipse with corner brackets and a live stats readout (rotation, dolly, FOV, state); it shatters into flying triangles, then reassembles.
- 5.6-7.2s: Systems: the shape becomes a dense red particle diamond, explodes into curling flow-field streams, then the particles settle into a large speckled word over a red rule and a small tracked caption.
- 7.5-9.2s: Grid: modular cards build on a 12-column grid: a big red card counting frames up to 900, a palette swatch card, a type specimen card, three easing-curve thumbnails and a tempo card with a red bar chart.
- 9.4-11s: Transitions, one per ~0.5 s with labels beneath: a horizontally blurred red whip word, a spinning red hexagon mask with a rotated word, then a word with outlined echo copies stepping above and below.
- 11.3-15s: Identity: red diamonds scatter around a thin orbit circle and converge into the logo diamond beside the heavy cream name; a red dotted rule with meta stats draws under it, then a two-tone tagline and a small Chinese translation line fade in and hold.

- الخط: غروتسك عريض فائق السواد للعرض، وخط أحادي تقني للتعليقات والوسوم والبيانات؛ وكلمات العرض غالباً بنسخ صدى أو نسخ مفرغة.

## Motion rules
- Persistent editor chrome: a red timeline with keyframe diamonds and a playhead along the bottom, a code-comment line top-left, chapter number and name top-right, timecode and BPM readouts, thin corner brackets.
- Seven chapters in 15 s (~2 s each), cut on the 128 BPM grid; inside a chapter, sub-beats land on eighth notes (~0.23 s).
- Easing is the hero: show the curve, then use it; strong ease-in-out and back-out overshoots, with heavy directional motion blur on whips.
- Procedural set-pieces (shatter/reassemble mesh, curl-noise particles forming text) stay inside the red/cream palette.
- Palette: ink #0d0b0a, warm black #1a1412, red-orange #e8402a, bone cream #ece6dc, grey #8a8580.
- Type: an ultra-black wide grotesk for display, a technical monospace for comments, labels and stats; display words often get echo or outline duplicates.
