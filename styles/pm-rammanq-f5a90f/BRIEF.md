## الفكرة
الفيلم يعرض اتساع المنتج بسرعة: كل ميزة فصل قصير مرقم (01 المحفظة حتى 12 الهاتف) بنفس القالب: وسم صغير أزرق متباعد، عنوان من سطرين نصفه أسود ونصفه أزرق، رسم بياني حي يُبنى أمامك، وجملة أرقام حقيقية في الأسفل بكلمة زرقاء. تبديل الخلفية بين الفاتح والأزرق والكحلي الداكن يمنع الملل ويعطي لكل فصل مزاجه (الأزمات على الأسود، الأرباح على الأزرق).

## Concept
The film shows how wide the product is, fast: each feature is a short numbered chapter (01 portfolio to 12 phone) on the same template: a small tracked blue kicker, a two-line headline half black and half blue, a live chart building in front of you, and a sentence of real numbers at the bottom with a blue phrase. Switching backgrounds between light, blue and dark navy keeps it moving and gives each chapter a mood (crashes on black, dividends on blue).

- 3-5s: 'All your investments. One view.': a blue area line chart draws left to right with logos popping on its points, a portfolio total counting up and a time-range toggle.
- 5-8.5s: Allocation donut sweeps in by sector with a legend whose percentages count; then holdings bars grow per company with percentages.
- 8.5-12.2s: The holdings fade to show what is inside a fund, then cut to a full blue background: a month calendar where dividend chips pop onto dates and a 12-month forecast counts.
- 12.2-17s: 'You vs the market': two lines race from 2016, the blue portfolio ending well above a grey index line, with small metric chips (alpha, Sharpe, beta).
- 17-24s: Dark navy: 'What if 2008 happened again?' two falling lines with the gap shaded and crash chips; then '1,000 possible futures' as hundreds of thin blue paths fan out from today's value; then a light efficient-frontier scatter where dots fly in and a curve draws to a highlighted best mix.

- 33-42s: Blue background: 'In your pocket. Just ask.' with a phone mock rising in and a chat bubble answer; end on light grey with the app icon in a soft circle, name, tagline, a blue 'try it free' button and platform list.

## Motion rules
- Chapter template: tracked uppercase kicker 'NN TOPIC' in blue, a two-line headline (line one near-black #111827, line two blue #2f7fc1), the chart, and a one-sentence caption with a blue highlighted phrase.
- Backgrounds rotate by mood: light #eef1f5, brand blue #3a86c8 with white type, dark navy #0e131c with white and blue type.
- Data colours: blue #2f7fc1 primary, grey #9aa3ad benchmark, green #22a06b gains, coral #f06a5d losses, orange #f39c12 highlights and breakevens.
- Type: heavy tight grotesk for headlines and big numbers (negative tracking), regular sans for captions, tabular numerals for counters.
- Headlines rise per line with a mask reveal (~300 ms, ~120 ms stagger); captions type or fade word by word after the chart settles.
- Charts always build: lines draw with a glowing head dot, donuts sweep clockwise, bars grow from zero, scatter dots fly in, counters tick to their final value.
- Each chapter lasts about 2-4 s with quick fades or hard cuts between them; a persistent small logo sits top-right throughout.
