## الفكرة
الموضوع تطبيق يحلل أخبار السوق والأرباح بالذكاء الاصطناعي. الفيلم مبني كجولة سريعة في خمس ميزات مرقمة، وكل ميزة تُختصر في عنوان من سطرين (السطر الثاني بالبنفسجي) وبطاقة واجهة حقيقية الشكل تنبثق وتتحرك فيها الأرقام. شريط تبويب سفلي ثابت يضيء القسم الحالي فيعطي إحساس التنقل داخل التطبيق.

## Concept
The subject is an app that uses AI to analyse market news and earnings. The film is a quick tour of five numbered features, each reduced to a two-line headline (second line in violet) and a realistic UI card that pops in with numbers animating inside it. A fixed bottom tab bar lights the current section, so the whole piece feels like navigating inside the app.

## Motion rules
- Ground: deep navy #0d0d24 to #15143a with a soft violet radial glow; accent violet #6b5cf0; cards white #ffffff with light grey text; gain green #22c55e.
- Headlines: heavy rounded grotesk, line one white, line two violet, set top-left under a thin rule plus numbered mono kicker ('01 · …').
- UI cards float with soft shadows, enter with a 300-400 ms scale-and-slide and settle with slight overshoot; numbers inside count up with a rolling-digit effect.
- A persistent bottom tab bar in tiny mono caps highlights the active section in violet.
- Transitions vary per section: violet diagonal wipe, full-frame violet flash, and blurred tilted fragments snapping into a grid.
- Ticker tapes are slanted ~4° and scroll continuously; the punch words use a brief 2-3 frame RGB split.
- About one beat every 0.6 s in the opener and a new section every ~2 s.
