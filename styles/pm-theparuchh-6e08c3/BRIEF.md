## Concept
The idea is that the sun in this world is not a star but something else. The teaser builds mystery slowly: calm wide shots in dusk light, a ring of light around a black disc recurring in every shot like an icon, then a gradual blackout with flying embers, until the ring is revealed as part of a winged mechanical creature in flight. There is no text at all until the title card.
