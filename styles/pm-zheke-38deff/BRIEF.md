## الفكرة
الموضوع تحدٍّ مفتوح: أثبت أنك تتقن الموشن. الجواب جملة واحدة تتكرر، «أستطيع» ثم فعل، وكل فعل يفعل ما يقوله: الكلمة تنضغط، أو تتجاوز ثم ترتد، أو تتحول إلى جسم ثلاثي الأبعاد، أو تنكسر خلف زجاج، أو تتمدد. القطع سريع جداً على شبكة إيقاع ثابتة، والحقول اللونية تتبدل مع كل قطعة، والكرة البرتقالية الصغيرة هي الخيط الذي يعود في النقطة الختامية.

## Concept
The subject is an open challenge: prove you can do motion design. The answer is one repeated sentence, 'I can' plus a verb, and each verb does what it says: the word squashes, overshoots and springs back, becomes a 3D object, refracts behind glass or stretches. Cutting is very fast on a fixed beat grid, colour fields change with every cut, and the small orange ball is the thread that returns as the final full stop.

## Motion rules
- Cut on a fixed beat grid of about 0.47 s (roughly 128 BPM): around 26 hard cuts, never a dissolve, with a stutter burst near 10.4-11 s.
- Every verb is demonstrated literally by its own animation: squash and stretch scale, spring overshoot, 3D render, refraction, vertical stretch with RGB split, strike-through.
- Layout constant: a small 'I CAN' label top-left, the verb filling the square full-bleed below it.
- One recurring prop: the orange ball bounces in the opening and returns as the full stop in the last two cards.
- Static camera except the square tunnel zoom; zero holds, the frame is always moving.
- Palette: cobalt #2b37f4, cream #ece9de, near-black #0d0d0f, orange #ff5a1f and violet #8f49c3 in the liquid backdrop.
- Type: condensed heavy grotesk 900 uppercase only; render square 1080x1080 at 60 fps.
