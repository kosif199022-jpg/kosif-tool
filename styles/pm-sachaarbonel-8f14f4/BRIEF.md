## الفكرة
الموضوع مساعد يأخذ وصف النتيجة بجملة واحدة ويتولى العمل المتكرر وحده. النبرة هادئة وواثقة كإعلان منتج راقٍ: كل قسم وسم صغير وعنوان من سطرين وجملة شرح، وبجانبه بطاقة واجهة واحدة أو اثنتان تتحرك بخفة. التحول الوحيد في الإضاءة هو الليل: الخلفية تصبح كحلية والساعة تتقدم لتبين أن العمل يحدث أثناء النوم.

## Concept
The subject is an assistant that takes a one-sentence description of an outcome and handles the recurring work on its own. The tone is calm and confident like a premium product ad: each section has a small kicker, a two-line headline and one explanatory sentence, beside one or two UI cards moving gently. The only lighting change is night: the background turns navy and a clock advances to show the work happening while you sleep.

- 4.5-9 ث: الشعار في الوسط (علامة قوس متدرجة واسم بأحرف صغيرة) فوق وعد من سطرين، الثاني مائل.

- 24-36 ث: بطاقة ملخص الصباح بسؤال واحد مميز يُجاب بنقرة؛ بطاقة تقرير منجز بمصادره؛ ثم انتقال ضبابي إلى قائمة إعدادات بمفاتيح تحدد ما يحتاج موافقة.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-4.5s: Pastel blur with a faint UI ghost: a kicker and three serif lines stack about the same reports and price checks, the third in grey italic.
- 4.5-9s: Centred logo (a gradient arch mark and lowercase wordmark) over a two-line promise, the second line italic.
- 9-19s: Left-text, right-card layout: a prompt box types a weekly request and replies with a setup confirmation; then a tilted plan card with steps, schedule and a preview card slides in.
- 19-24s: Night: background darkens to navy-violet, a huge clock counts from about 01:19 to 06:59 while job rows tick to done on the right.
- 24-36s: Morning summary card with one highlighted question that gets answered with a click; a finished report card with sources; a blurred transition into a settings list with toggles about what needs approval.
- 36-49s: Team access card with roles and an activity feed; pricing card with range numbers counting and a usage bar; a version-history card with code and a timeline; each change uses a soft blur cross-fade.
- 49-59s: Blur out to the end card: logo, two-line serif tagline with the second line italic, a dark waitlist button and the domain; long hold.

## Motion rules
- Background: a slowly drifting mesh gradient of cream #f5ebdb, peach #f6d9c4 and lavender #ddd6f0; night section navy-violet #2c2c3b to #4a4566.
- Type: a soft text serif in deep navy #2b2f7a for headlines with the second line italic; tiny orange-dotted uppercase kickers; small grey sans for body.
- UI cards are white #ffffff with 12-16 px radius, soft long shadows and a slight 3D tilt (about 5-10 degrees) that eases toward flat as they settle.
- Section transitions are soft: heavy gaussian blur plus cross-fade over about 600 ms; no hard cuts.
- In-card motion is literal product use: typing in the prompt box, check marks turning green, a cursor clicking one button, numbers counting.
- Slow pacing: about 5-7 s per section with long readable holds; the end card holds for about 8 s.
