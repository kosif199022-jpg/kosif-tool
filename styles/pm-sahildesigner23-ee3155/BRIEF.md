## الفكرة
الفكرة رحلة في 'مراحل' موشن ديزاينر: تايبو حركي، إيقاع، نص ثلاثي الأبعاد، محاكاة سوائل، منتج، توليدي، مونتاج، ثم التوقيع. كل مرحلة مرقمة في تسمية صغيرة بالأسفل، والبرتقالي هو الخيط الوحيد بين كل المشاهد، والنهاية تكشف أن كل شيء صُنع في محرر واحد.

## Concept
A walk through a motion designer's 'chapters': kinetic type, rhythm, 3D type, fluid simulation, product, generative, edit, then the signature. Each chapter is numbered in a small bottom label, orange is the single thread through every shot, and the ending reveals everything living in one editor timeline.

- 11.5-13 ث: ابتعاد إلى واجهة محرر: قائمة طبقات يساراً والطبقة النشطة بالأحمر، منحنى محرر الرسوم أعلى اليمين، خط زمني بمقاطع الفصول وموجة صوت رمادية؛ والمعاينة تعرض الكلمة ثم دائرة رادار.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Black: a thin cream rule draws across, splits into two rules and the word appears between them, its letters briefly tinting peach-orange one after another.
- 1.8-3.8s: Rows of outline repeats of the word scroll in opposite directions behind the solid white word, fading from red outline to dark emboss.
- 3.8-5.6s: The word becomes chunky 3D type with orange extrusion, tilts and swings, then pushes past camera to the right.
- 5.6-7.6s: Flat orange ground: four chrome metaballs with orange reflections merge and split; one stretches into a chrome pillar; a thin callout line labels it.
- 7.6-9.2s: Cream ground: a black phone mockup shows a render screen counting up a percentage while an orange pixel grid fills; camera pushes into the screen.
- 9.2-11.5s: Through the screen into a dark 3D field of extruded cubes where orange blocks rise into a heart shape and white cubes form a ring.
- 11.5-13s: Pull out to an editor UI: layer list on the left with the active layer in red, a graph-editor curve top-right, a timeline with chapter clips and a grey waveform; the preview shows the word, then a radar circle.
- 13-15s: Cream end card: a geometric bold sans name rises letter by letter with an orange dot and a thin orbit circle, small mono subtitle and footer line.

## Motion rules
- Chapters of ~1.5-2 s, each labelled bottom-left with a tiny number and name; persistent HUD of timecode top-right, a project label top-left and BPM/aspect readouts bottom-right.
- Palette: near-black #111111 / #1a1a1a, signal orange #ff4f1f, peach #f4a48a, cream #efeae3, chrome greys #c8c8c8 to #5a5a5a; red #e0322b only for the active layer in the editor.
- Type: a heavy geometric/grotesk sans for the hero word and name (tight tracking), tiny monospace for every label.
- Transitions are continuous camera moves: push-through the phone screen into 3D, pull-out from the scene into the editor preview.
- 3D type and cubes use soft studio lighting with a single key light and contact shadows; metaballs are glossy chrome with an orange environment.
- Letter-by-letter reveals with ~40 ms stagger and slight upward travel; blocks rise with staggered spring motion.
