## الفكرة
الخدمة تَعِد بأن المسافر لا يفعل إلا حجز رحلته وهي تتولى كل ما بعد ذلك. لذلك يُبنى الفيلم على رمزين بسيطين: تذكرة الطيران (المهمة الوحيدة للعميل) وحقيبة السفر (التي تبقى ثابتة بينما تُنجز الإجراءات فوقها واحداً تلو الآخر). أزرق سماوي واحد مع أبيض وكحلي داكن يمنح إحساس الثقة والهدوء، والخط عريض مستدير ودود، والإيقاع سريع ومنظم كقائمة مهام تُشطب.

## Concept
The service promises the traveller only has to book the flight while it handles everything after. So the film is built on two simple symbols: the boarding pass (the client's one job) and the suitcase (which stays still while procedures are completed above it one by one). A single sky blue with white and dark navy gives a calm, trustworthy feel, the type is a friendly rounded bold sans, and the rhythm is quick and orderly like a to-do list being ticked off.

## Motion rules
- Palette: sky blue #1a9bf0, pale ground #f2f8fd, white #ffffff, dark navy #121a2c for the suitcase and card backdrop, ink #14161c, soft blue glow #cfe6fb.
- Type: heavy rounded geometric sans (Plus Jakarta / Manrope ExtraBold style) with tight tracking; small tracked uppercase kickers; key words recoloured blue and sometimes underlined with a hand-drawn stroke.
- Line-by-line writes: each headline line rises from a mask over about 0.3 s, staggered 0.15 s; one line uses letters that tumble and rotate into place.
- The suitcase is the anchor: it never moves during the checklist while the pill above swaps with a quick vertical slide and the progress dots fill one per step.
- Transitions: a hard colour switch, a circular iris with an outline ring, a diagonal multi-bar wipe, and a photo-to-card shrink; cuts land every ~0.6 s in the checklist and photo runs.
- Photos get a slow 1.05x push-in and a dark bottom gradient so the white names read.
