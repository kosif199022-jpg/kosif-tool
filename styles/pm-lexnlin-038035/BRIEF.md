## الفكرة
الفكرة أن «التفكير» إرث إنساني طويل، فيمر رمز الشعار عبر تاريخ الفضول البصري: نقوش عصر النهضة، صور العلوم الطبيعية، دراسات الحركة، النحت. اللون الوحيد هو البرتقالي المحمر للنجمة وأثرها، وكل ما سواه أبيض وأسود، فيبدو الرمز كأنه يد تشير وتربط بين الصور. الكلمات قليلة جداً، كلمة واحدة لكل فكرة.

## Concept
The idea is that thinking is a long human inheritance, so the logo mark travels through a history of visual curiosity: Renaissance engravings, natural-science photographs, motion studies, sculpture. The only colour is the coral of the star and its trail; everything else is black and white, so the mark reads like a pointing hand connecting the images. Words are very few, one per idea.

## Motion rules
- Two-colour discipline: white #ffffff ground, black #141414 ink and archive images in greyscale; the only accent is coral #d97757 for the star, strokes and dots.
- Type: a heavy old-style serif for single words and the wordmark, black, centred, with generous white space; no secondary type.
- Archive images are masked cut-outs or tilted prints placed off-centre; each holds 0.5-0.8 s with a hard cut or a slight scale settle.
- The coral mark is the connective motion: it moves as a brush stroke, a dot riding a drawn arc, or a needle, linking one image to the next.
- Black line arcs and spirals draw on with steady speed (about 600-900 ms) and often carry the eye into the next cut.
- Pacing: very fast montage (about 2 cuts per second) in the middle, slowing for the spiral and the final logo hold.
- The starburst is shown in three treatments: flat icon, extruded 3D, and smeared brush blob, before resolving to flat.
