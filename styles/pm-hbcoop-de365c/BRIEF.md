## الفكرة
الفكرة كتاب علمي قديم يُقلّب صفحاته: كل «شكل» درس هندسي يُبنى على الذي قبله، من أبسط عنصر (نقطة) إلى عالم كامل، ثم يعود كل شيء إلى نقطة واحدة تختم الجملة الأخيرة. اللغة البصرية متقشفة: ورق، حبر أسود، لون أحمر واحد للتعليقات والقياسات، وتعليق مائل صغير يُكتب تحت كل مرحلة. الانتقال بين الأشكال تحوّل مستمر للجسم نفسه وليس قطعاً، فيبدو الفيلم كلقطة واحدة.

## Concept
It plays like an old science book turning its plates: each 'figure' is a geometry lesson built on the last, from the simplest element (a point) to a whole world, after which everything returns to a single dot that closes the final sentence. The visual language is austere: paper, black ink, one red for annotations and measurements, and a small italic caption typed under each stage. Shapes morph continuously rather than cutting, so the film reads as one shot.

- 4.5-7 s: The line sweeps an arc around one end to draw a full circle with a diameter and a small red 'd' label; FIG. 3 / FIG. 4.

## Motion rules
- Paper ground #efe9de with a faint fibre texture and soft vignette; ink black #1a1a1a for all shapes; one red #c8402e for the cursor, annotations, measurement labels and trails; shadows are soft warm grey at ~25% opacity.
- One continuous object: every figure morphs out of the previous one in place (point to line to circle to sphere to lens to ball to flock to globe to dot); no hard cuts in the whole film.
- Captions: small-caps red 'FIG. N' followed by an italic serif noun, centred at the bottom, typed on with a blinking cursor at each new stage; a tiny letter-spaced credit sits top-right throughout.
- Physics feels real but slow: the bounce follows true gravity with a restitution of about 0.75 and the dotted trail records each frame position; the flock uses boids-style flow with thousands of points.
- 3D stages use a high, soft key light from top-left, glossy reflections on the black sphere and refraction for the glass stage; camera is mostly locked with a gentle tilt into perspective.
- Each figure holds ~2-4 s; the final sentence is set in a classic book serif at large size, typed character by character, and the dot lands as its full stop.
