## الفكرة
الفكرة: «أين نذهب؟» سؤال يومي، والتطبيق يجيب بالبيانات الحقيقية للمجتمع. كل رقم يتحول إلى نقاط ترمز إلى أماكن أو تقييمات، والنقاط نفسها تتشكل وتتفكك بين المشاهد. ثلاثة أسطح متناوبة: أسود دافئ للبيانات والتطبيق، كريمي للحياة اليومية، وبرتقالي للأسئلة والختام، مع إطار بزوايا وتوقيع صغير ثابت على كل الإطارات.

## Concept
The idea: 'where should we go?' is a daily question, and the app answers with real community data. Every number becomes dots standing for places or reviews, and the dots themselves form and dissolve between scenes. Three alternating surfaces carry it: warm black for data and the app, cream for everyday life, and orange for questions and the finale, all inside a corner-bracket frame with a small constant brand tag.

## Motion rules
- Three surfaces: warm black #14110f with an orange radial glow, cream #f4f1ea, signal orange #ee5a0a; ink black #111111 and white #f7f5f0; gold #d9a43a only for badges.
- Type system: heavy condensed sans for numbers and questions, italic serif for labels and taglines, tiny tracked mono caps for kickers and footnotes, rounded heavy caps for the wordmark.
- Numbers count up with easing while their dot visual builds; dots are the connective tissue and morph between sphere, grid, map, shape and ring rather than cutting.
- Every frame sits inside thin corner brackets with a small arrow-plus-name tag top-left and a date/source note bottom-right.
- Questions roll vertically with motion blur (~300 ms per swap); serif lines build word by word with a slight letter-by-letter rotation.
- Isometric rooms slide horizontally like a carousel, each held about 2.5 s with an orange progress line filling.
- Few hard cuts (around 1.5 s, 4.5 s, 9 s); most transitions are morphs, slides and surface flips; the finale is a star-field warp into the logo.
