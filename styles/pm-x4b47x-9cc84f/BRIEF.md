## الفكرة
الفكرة تأمل هادئ في الفرق بين البيت كمبنى والبيت كمكان ينتظرك. كل شيء يُروى بصور ظلية بسيطة على خشبة مسرح صغير، والزمن يتقدم بالسماء وحدها: من الغروب إلى الليل، ثم يوم آخر بشجرة خريفية، ثم ليل مرصع بالنجوم والقرية كلها مضاءة. اللافتة المعلقة تحمل جملة قصيرة في كل مقطع، كأنها راوٍ صامت، وتنتهي الحكاية بانغلاق الستارة.

## Concept
The idea is a quiet reflection on the difference between a house as a building and a home as a place that waits for you. Everything is told with simple silhouettes on a small theatre stage, and time moves only through the sky: sunset into night, then another day with an autumn tree, then a star-filled night with the whole village lit. A hanging sign carries one short line per section like a silent narrator, and the tale ends as the curtain closes.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-2s: Red velvet curtains fill the frame with a gold seam of light down the middle, then part to the sides under a scalloped valance.
- 2-7s: Orange sunset sky with a pale low sun, layered silhouette hills, trees, a fence and a street lamp; a dark sign hangs on two strings at the top and its first line writes in.
- 7-12s: The house silhouette rises into place from below, and a man in a hat and scarf walks in from the left and stops; the sign swaps to a new line.
- 12-19s: The sky blends to pink dusk then violet; the moon rises and the house windows warm up to glowing yellow one by one.
- 19-31s: Night: a curling line of scent drifts from the chimney, then the door opens with a warm light spilling a path on the ground and the man steps inside; the sign changes twice.
- 31-40s: A new day in pale cream light: a bare autumn tree with a swing appears at left, birds cross, leaves fall and the sky slowly dims back toward dusk.
- 40-54s: Deep violet starry night: the camera slowly pulls back, the house shrinks into a village of lit windows with fireflies along the ground, and the closing lines appear on the sign.
- 54-57s: The curtains sweep back in from both sides and close.
