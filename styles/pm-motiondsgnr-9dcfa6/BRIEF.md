## الفكرة
الفكرة شوريل يقول «أستطيع فعل كل شيء» في ربع دقيقة: كل فصل تقنية مختلفة تماماً (تايبوغرافي، جسيمات، أشكال، سوائل، حلقات، بيانات، فن توليدي، تركيب)، والذي يوحدها هو لوحة ألوان أساسية جريئة وخط غروتسك ثقيل وواجهة عرض صغيرة تكرر رقم الفصل واسمه ودقة الإطار. الختام يجمع كل اللقطات في شبكة ليقول «كل ذلك».

## Concept
The idea is a reel that says 'I can do it all' in a quarter minute: each chapter is a completely different technique (type, particles, shapes, fluids, rings, data, generative art, compositing), held together by a bold primary palette, one heavy grotesk and a small HUD that repeats chapter number, name and frame spec. The finale tiles every shot into a grid to say 'all of it'.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.2s: Black screen with a single orange dot, which bursts to a full orange ground where a heavy black word wobbles letter by letter with a tiny mono subtitle.
- 1.2-3s: Royal blue ground with a faint repeated outline-text pattern scrolling behind; a three-line slogan stacks in (white, yellow, white) with an orange underline, then exits with heavy horizontal motion blur.
- 3-4.2s: Black: a dotted particle sphere with orbit rings rotates, then its particles scatter and reform as a name in red-violet speckle.
- 4.2-5.5s: Cream ground: a large orange disc with orbiting dots inside a dotted circle morphs into a rounded blue square that spins with motion blur.
- 5.5-7.3s: A soft yellow disc floods the frame; a gooey blue liquid blob with black holes ripples behind a two-tone headline, which fades to a ghost.
- 7.3-8.7s: A tunnel of concentric circular text rings in orange, white, blue and pink spins in alternating directions while the camera zooms through it.
- 8.7-11.6s: Cream ground: a headline then three rounded cards (blue bar chart, black easing curve, orange FPS gauge counting up); then black with stacked orange-to-blue waveform ridgelines under spaced caps.
- 11.6-15s: A grid of tiles replaying every chapter assembles, a big white slogan with orange dot overlays it, then a black end card with a multicolour wordmark, floating triangles, circles and squares, a role line typing in and an availability pill.

## Motion rules
- Primary palette: black #0e0f12, cream #f2efe6, orange #ff5a1f, royal blue #2f4bff, yellow #ffc21a, plus pink #ff6fa8 and green #22c08a only in the final wordmark.
- Type: heavy neo-grotesk caps for all headlines, tiny spaced monospace for HUD labels; one grid of ~5% margins.
- Persistent HUD in every chapter: chapter number and name bottom-left, frame spec bottom-right, a running timecode top-right and thin corner brackets.
- One technique per chapter, ~1-2 s each, joined by hard cuts or a colour flood; never two techniques on screen until the compositing finale.
- Strong directional motion blur on exits and fast spins; elastic overshoot on type entrances; particles and liquid move continuously.
- Data cards use rounded 16 px corners on solid colour fills and animate their numbers and bars together.
- The finale grid replays live thumbnails of earlier chapters rather than still images.
