## الفكرة
الموضوع منصة تجعل نماذج اللغة الكبيرة تتبارى في ألعاب الورق والرقعة. الفيديو بنية عرض تقديمي منظم: كل قسم يبدأ بوسم صغير مرقم وعنوان من جملتين (الثانية بالذهبي) وسطر رمادي، ثم واجهة المنتج الحقيقية تُبنى تحته قطعة قطعة. لا ممثلين ولا قطعات سريعة، بل تتابع هادئ يبيع الشفافية والعدالة: البيانات نفسها لكل نموذج وكل خطوة مسجلة.

## Concept
The subject is a platform where large language models compete at card and board games. The video is a tidy presentation: each section opens with a small numbered kicker, a two-clause headline (the second in gold) and a grey subline, then the real product UI builds beneath piece by piece. No actors and no fast cuts, just a calm sequence that sells fairness and transparency: every model gets the same data and every move is recorded.

## Motion rules

- Type: a bold CJK sans for headlines (white then gold clause), small grey sublines, tiny gold pill kickers numbered like '01 - ...', monospace in the code panel.
- Every section follows the same template: kicker pops, headline fades up, subline fades, then UI pieces enter with staggered 80-120 ms fade-and-rise.
- Sections change with a soft fade through the dark background, never hard cuts; roughly 8 s per section.
- Product UI is rebuilt as clean vector components (cards, pills, boards, forms), not screenshots; model badges are coloured circles with short abbreviations.
- Connector lines draw on as glowing curves with travelling dots; game boards update one move at a time in sync with the thinking cards.
