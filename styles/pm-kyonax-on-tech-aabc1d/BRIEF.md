## الفكرة
فيلم يشرح خط إنتاج الأداة كما يراه المطور: تكتب ملفاً نصياً واحداً، فيمر بأربع مراحل، ويخرج موقعاً كاملاً بكل ملفاته. كل شيء داخل شاشة داكنة كطرفية فاخرة، بلون تأكيد واحد أصفر ذهبي، ونصوص كود حقيقية تظهر بتأثير فك الترميز، وأرقام مراحل ضخمة تملأ اليمين كأنها لوحة عدادات. التميمة البكسلية تفتتح وتختم لتعطي الأداة شخصية.

## Concept
A film that explains the tool's pipeline as a developer sees it: write one text file, it passes four stages and comes out as a complete site with all its files. Everything lives on a dark screen like a premium terminal with one golden yellow accent, real code text appearing with a decode effect, and huge stage numerals filling the right side like a dashboard. The pixel mascot opens and closes the film to give the tool a personality.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Black screen with a glitchy horizontal scanline flicker; a dot-matrix animal-head mascot assembles in white pixels, then its pixels break into falling yellow and white glyphs.
- 1.8-3.7s: A framed code editor at left decodes an Org document line by line with line numbers; at right a two-line headline appears white with the last word yellow; a terminal pill beneath types a build command.
- 3.7-7.4s: Stage run: a yellow scan bar sweeps one editor line; on the right a huge yellow numeral with a short uppercase label swaps through four stages about 1 s each; the left panel morphs accordingly into token tags, an AST tree with elbow connectors, HTML tags decoding, and a rendered article preview with a row of colour swatches.
- 7.4-9.2s: Metadata and theming: the page gets annotated with leader lines to small labels; a short headline pair says one flag gives a new look while a terminal types a style flag and the preview flips from dark to light.
- 9.4-10.9s: Outputs: a headline over a grid of file cards (page, metadata, structured data, sitemap, feed, routes) decoding their contents, with a row of small stats below.
- 11.3-13s: Use cases: three quick beats, each a large white word at left beside a dim screen (a blog, an app, a pipeline in a terminal).
- 13.1-15s: End card: the yellow pixel mascot at left, the wordmark with its middle character in yellow, a terminal pill typing the install command, and small credit lines.

- Cadence: stage swaps about every 1 s, use-case beats about 0.6 s each, two hard cuts (about 3.7 s and 13.1 s), everything else morphs within the same layout.
