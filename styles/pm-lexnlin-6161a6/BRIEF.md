## الفكرة
البطل هو مؤشر الكتابة: يبدأ ككاتب نص ثم «يتوقف عن الكتابة ويبدأ الإخراج»، فيقفز بين البطاقات والقوائم والخط الزمني ويقود كل انتقال. الفيلم أبيض نظيف بخط بلا زوائد ولمسة زرقاء واحدة، والأمثلة تمر كشريط أفلام على خط زمني تحته وسم قصير لكل نوع.

## Concept
The hero is the text caret: it starts as a writer, then 'stops typing and directs', jumping between cards, lists and the timeline and driving every transition. The film is clean white with a sans and one blue accent, and the sample films pass like a filmstrip over a ruler-style timeline with a short label for each kind.

## Motion rules
- Palette: near-white #f7f8fa to light grey #eceef1 backgrounds, ink #16181d, one caret blue #2f6fd6; sample films bring their own colours (brick red #c8343a, mint #dcefe3) only inside their cards.
- The caret is the director: a 2-3 px blue bar that blinks at 530 ms, types at about 20 chars/s, and moves to the next focus before each transition.
- Type: neutral grotesk for copy (semibold headlines, regular body), monospace for effect chips, terminal and command pill.
- Transitions are object-led, not cuts: cards split, scale up, slide on a carousel with motion blur and a slight 3D tilt; about 300-500 ms with ease-in-out.
- A ruler timeline with a blue playhead sits under every sample card and advances as the carousel moves.
- Chips fall with light physics (rotation and settle) and hold as texture under the text.
