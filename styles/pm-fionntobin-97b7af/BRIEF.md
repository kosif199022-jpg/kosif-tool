## الفكرة
الفكرة أن كل إطار مكتوب بالكود، فيعرض الشوريل ثمانية «أشكال» مرقمة (شبكة، خط، بيانات، شخص، فضاء، نقاط، نظام، توقيع) بإيقاع الموسيقى. الطابع مجلة تصميم: لونان فقط، شبكة رفيعة ظاهرة، أرقام صفحات صغيرة في الزوايا، ومزج بين سيريف كلاسيكي للأرقام والتوقيع، ومكثف عريض للكلمات الكبيرة، وسيريف مائل للهمس.

## Concept
The idea is that every frame is written in code, so the reel shows eight numbered 'figures' (grid, type, data, figure, space, dots, system, signature) on the music's beat. It looks like a design magazine: two colours only, a visible thin grid, tiny page numbers in the corners, and a mix of classic serif for numerals and the signature, wide condensed grotesk for the big words, and italic serif for the asides.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-1.8s: Grid: on cream with a faint layout grid, blue squares fill cells in doubling steps (2, 8, 64) while a large black serif number in the bottom-right counts with them.
- 1.8-3.5s: Type: hard cut to full cobalt; 'EVERY' in a tall condensed face fills the frame, shrinks to the corner, an italic serif 'frame' appears, a word is struck through, and a heavy wide 'written.' lands at the bottom.
- 3.5-5.5s: Data: cream again; a serif BPM number ticks up while a blue step chart draws across the frame with beat markers and small callouts on the kick.
- 5.5-7.2s: Figure: a blue stick runner sprints and leaps blue hurdles along a baseline, one pose shown with construction guide lines and a circle.
- 7.2-9.2s: Space: an isometric field of blue tiles extrudes in a wave, then the camera flattens to a straight-on grid of squares.
- 9.2-11s: Dots: the squares shrink to a dense dot grid, then halftone ripples spread from centres with an overprinted dark ring.
- 11-12.8s: System: cobalt matrix of small glyph variants with a path linking chosen cells and a large cream glyph assembling on the right; a single-frame black flash with the glyph.
- 12.8-15s: Signature: a huge blue serif name with a square full stop on cream over the grid, then small credits lines appear above it.

## Motion rules
- Strict two-colour system: cream #eeebe0 and cobalt #2b27c9, with ink #0c0c1a only for small text and one flash frame.
- Cut on the beat: eight figures in 15 s, about 1.8 s each; inside a figure, elements step in time with the music (doubling, step chart, hurdles).
- Editorial furniture on every frame: a thin layout grid, tiny top-left title, top-right figure number 'n / 08', bottom-left section name, bottom-right frame counter.
- Type mix: classic text serif for numerals and the signature, tall condensed and wide heavy grotesk for statement words, italic serif for soft words, monospace for annotations.
- Geometry first: squares, dots, tiles and glyphs are built from the same grid unit so each figure morphs naturally into the next.
- Halftone and overprint textures are drawn procedurally (dot screens at an angle) rather than with images.
