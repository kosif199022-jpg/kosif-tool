## الفكرة
الموضوع رسومات يدوية حقيقية تُحرَّك لتروي يوماً كاملاً من الصباح إلى الليل. القصة قوس زمني: شروق على التلة، استيقاظ الحيوانات، حديقة، بحر، غروب، ليل، ثم احتفال ختامي يجمع الجميع. الأسلوب يحافظ على أصالة الورق: ملمس ألوان مائية، شخصيات مقصوصة كملصقات بحد أبيض، خط يدوي للكلمات، وأصوات مولدة برمجياً لكل حدث تظهر أحياناً مكتوبة بجانب الشخصية.

## Concept
The subject is real hand-drawn artwork brought to life to tell a whole day from morning to night. The story is a time arc: sunrise on the hill, the animals waking, a garden, the sea, sunset, night, then a closing celebration with everyone. The style keeps the paper authentic: watercolour texture, characters cut out as stickers with a white border, a handwritten face for words, and code-generated sounds for each event, sometimes written beside the character.

## Proposed scenes
- 0-10s: Calm intro on cream watercolour paper: a soft green hill across the lower third, a smiling sun slides in from the right and bobs, small line clouds and birds drift.
- 10-20s: Animals (bunny, birds, owl, fox, cat, chick, mantis) pop up from behind the hill as white-bordered stickers with overshoot, staggered about 150-250 ms, tiny written sound words beside them and a good-morning line.
- 20-33s: A rhythmic section: one handwritten word per shot over extreme macro push-ins into the drawings inside curved masks on flat light-blue and indigo fields, cuts about every 0.5-1 s and fastest around 27-31 s.
- 33-45s: Back on paper: flowers grow up from the baseline with a sunflower in the middle, butterflies and bees flutter on wavy paths, then faced vegetables and fruit pop in along the bottom row with bounce.
- 45-52s: Underwater: a blue gradient with light rays, jellyfish bobbing on sine motion, an octopus, fish swimming across and bubbles rising.
- 52-58s: A peach sunset wash with a setting sun, birds and a boat; a handwritten line types in letter by letter.
- 58-65s: Deep navy night: a tree house and a village whose windows switch on with a warm glow, fireflies floating, a moon idling, a shooting-star streak and a sweet-dreams line.
- 65-78s: Finale: pastel sunburst rays rotate on paper while every character gathers in an ellipse ring around a centred two-line handwritten title about being drawn by hand and brought to life.

## Motion rules
- Keep the paper real: watercolour texture under everything, characters as hand-drawn cut-outs with a white sticker border, colour washes for ground changes.
- Entrances are pops with overshoot from behind the horizon, staggered 150-250 ms; idle motion is gentle bobbing, drifting and sine-wave floating.
- Growth is from the root: flowers and plants scale up on Y from their base before insects start to flutter.
- One fast section only (about 20-33 s): one word per cut with aggressive macro push-ins; the rest of the film is calm with long holds.
- Every visible event gets a code-generated sound on the same frame (pop, chirp, bubble, twinkle), with occasional written onomatopoeia next to the source.
- Palette: paper #f9ecd3 and #e1d7ba, sage green #c6c7a4, sun yellow #f2c230, brown #bd9767, ink #1a1a1a, field blue #5a66b8, night navy #1c2349 and #273255.
- Type: a casual handwritten marker face in bold lowercase for all words; glow is used only for night windows, fireflies and the shooting star.
