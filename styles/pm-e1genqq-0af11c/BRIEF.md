## الفكرة
الفيديو عرض توضيحي للعملية لا للأسلوب فقط: يثبت أن الأداة تأخذ طلباً قصيراً وتنتج خطة ثم فيلماً منتهياً. لذلك يتناوب بين واجهة العمل الداكنة (محادثة وخطة ومستودع وطرفية) ونتيجة فاتحة هادئة. الفيلم الناتج نفسه أسلوب مستقل قابل للاستخدام: إعلان منتج إنتاجي بمساحات بيضاء دافئة، لون مميز واحد أخضر غامق، وتفاعل بسيط يشرح فكرة المنتج بتعليم المهام.

## Concept
The video demonstrates the process, not just a look: it proves the tool takes a short request, writes a plan, and outputs a finished film. So it alternates between the dark working interface (chat, plan, repository, terminal) and a light, calm result. The output film is a usable style of its own: a productivity product spot with warm off-white space, a single deep-green accent and a simple interaction that explains the product by ticking tasks.

## Motion rules
- Two worlds: the demo UI in dark browser greys (#1b1b1d background, #2a2a2e panels, light grey text) and the output film in cream #efeee6 with white cards #fbfaf5, ink #1f2a22 and one deep-green accent #23533a.
- Screen-recording grammar: real cursor movement, typed input, streaming text and slow scrolls; no zooms or added graphics on the demo parts.
- Output film type: a heavy neo-grotesque headline in two short stacked lines, left-aligned, with a small regular subline; card labels in a regular sans.
- Output film motion: cards drop in with a small rotation (about 3-5 degrees) and settle over about 0.5 s with soft ease; ticks happen one per beat (about 1 s apart); the green fill wipes across the last card.
- Lots of negative space and no background motion in the film; the call-to-action pill fades and rises in last, then the frame holds.
- Pacing: demo sections hold 8-20 s each while the viewer reads; the film segment plays its full sequence and then holds.
