## الفكرة
الموضوع أخطاء شائعة في صياغة الأسئلة للذكاء الاصطناعي، يُعرض كمقال مجلة يتحرك: صفحة واحدة بلون الورق، تايبوغرافي سيريف رفيع أنيق، وأحمر واحد للتنبيه وأخضر واحد للحل. كل فصل له قالب ثابت: بطاقة عنوان برقم مفرّغ ضخم ثم لوحة مقارنة 'ما يكتبه الناس / اكتب هذا بدلاً منه' مع الرد الحرفي تحت كل منهما وشريط 'الحل' في الأسفل.

## Concept
The subject is common mistakes in how people phrase questions to AI, presented as a moving magazine essay: one paper-coloured page, elegant thin serif typography, a single red for warnings and a single green for fixes. Each chapter follows a fixed template: a title card with a huge outlined numeral, then a comparison board of 'what people typed / type this instead', each with the verbatim reply beneath, and a 'fix' strip at the bottom.

## Motion rules
- One continuous paper page #ece6dc with a soft centre-light vignette; elements fade-and-rise 8-12 px into place (300-500 ms, ease-out) rather than cutting.
- Typography: a thin, condensed high-contrast serif for numerals and headlines (Didone-like), italic serif for accent words and subtitles, monospace for prompt text, tiny letterspaced caps for section labels.
- Two accent roles only: warning red #c8473f (outlines, highlights, icons) and fix green #2f7a4f (tags, bar); body ink #2b2724.
- Prompt cards are near-black #22201e with off-white monospace text; the loaded words get a red background highlight that wipes on left to right after the card lands.
- Chapter title cards hold ~6-8 s; numerals are outlined strokes ~2 px, icons drawn as single thin red lines.
- Numbers count up with tabular figures; the final bar grows over ~1 s with ease-out and the whisker appears after.
- Narration-length holds: boards stay still for many seconds while text is read.
