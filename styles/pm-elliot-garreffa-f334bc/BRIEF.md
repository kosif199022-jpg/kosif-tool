## الفكرة
المنتج يتابع أدوات الربط الجديدة في منظومات المساعدات الذكية، والفيلم يبنيه على ثلاثة أفعال: اكتشف، افهم، ابقَ على اطلاع. كل فصل مرقم بوسم صغير ويعرض لقطة واجهة نظيفة تتحرك فعلاً، والعنوان دائماً جملة واحدة نصفها أسود ونصفها أزرق. الخلفية الحية بنقاط خضراء متدرجة تعطي إحساس بيانات تتنفس دون أن تزاحم الواجهة.

## Concept
The product tracks new connectors across AI assistant ecosystems, and the film is built on three verbs: discover, understand, stay updated. Each chapter has a small numbered kicker and shows one clean UI moment that actually moves, and the headline is always one sentence, half black and half blue. A living background of dotted green gradients feels like breathing data without crowding the UI.

## Motion rules
- Palette: white #fbfcfb, royal blue #1652d9, ink #111418, dark green #0f2a22, halftone green blobs #9fd3a8 at low opacity; blue is the only accent.
- Headlines: tight-tracked medium grotesk, one sentence split two-tone (black then blue); they rise about 24 px and fade in over ~500 ms with an ease-out.
- Chapter kickers: tiny tracked monospace with a number and a slash, top-left, plus faint corner metadata on every frame.
- UI cards are white with 12 px radii and soft shadows; they enter tilted in 3D (10-15 degrees) and settle flat with a gentle overshoot.
- List rows and capability rows stagger in at about 150 ms intervals; connector lines draw on from the source card.
- The background halftone gradient drifts slowly the whole time; the big colour changes are a circular reveal wipe and fades, never hard cuts.
- Pace is calm: each chapter holds its finished state for 1-2 s before the next one fades in.
